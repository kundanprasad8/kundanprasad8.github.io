RRB NTPC Results

RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories) fills graduate and undergraduate posts like clerk, ticket collector, goods guard and station master across railway zones. Selection runs through CBT 1 (screening), CBT 2 (main), then typing/skill test or CBAT depending on the post, followed by document verification and medical.

How to Check the RRB NTPC Results

  1. Open your zone's RRB website (links listed on rrbcdg.gov.in) and find the NTPC result notice.
  2. Download the result PDF and search your roll number.
  3. Check the cut-off marks PDF for your zone and category.
  4. Note the next stage date — CBT 2, typing test or CBAT as applicable.
  5. Keep your graduation marksheet, caste certificate and ID ready for document verification.

Selection Stages

CBT 1 result shortlists candidates for CBT 2. CBT 2 result leads to the typing test (for clerk posts) or CBAT (for station master/goods guard), then document verification, medical and the final panel.

What to Do Next

CBT 2 is tougher than CBT 1 — restart preparation without waiting. Typing-post candidates should practice typing daily; the skill test has strict speed and accuracy limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the official RRB NTPC Results result declared?

On Railway Recruitment Board NTPC's official portal (rrbcdg.gov.in). Check only the official site — forwarded links and social-media posts are often outdated or fake.

What are the stages of the RRB NTPC Results selection?

CBT 1 result shortlists candidates for CBT 2. CBT 2 result leads to the typing test (for clerk posts) or CBAT (for station master/goods guard), then document verification, medical and the final panel.

I lost my registration number. How do I check my result?

Most portals let you recover it with your registered mobile number and date of birth. For PDF-only results, search your name — but confirm with your roll number later, since names repeat.

What is the cut-off and where do I find it?

The cut-off is the minimum marks needed to qualify for the next stage, released category-wise with (or shortly after) each result. It is published as a separate notice or inside the result PDF.

How long after the exam is the RRB NTPC Results result declared?

It varies by body and stage — typically 1 to 4 months for written exams. The commission usually gives no advance date; the result simply appears on the portal one day.

What should I do after qualifying?

CBT 2 is tougher than CBT 1 — restart preparation without waiting. Typing-post candidates should practice typing daily; the skill test has strict speed and accuracy limits.
