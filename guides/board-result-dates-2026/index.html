Board Result 2026 Dates: Expected Schedule

Published: 2026-10-12

Latest Update: 2026 board exams are over for most boards; results typically arrive April-June. Dates below are expected based on past years — official notice is the final word. Last Updated On: 13 October 2026

"Result kab aayega?" is the most asked question of every exam season. No site can give you the exact date before the board announces it — but past-year patterns tell you the expected window for each board.

At a Glance: Most Indian boards declare Class 10 and 12 results between April and June. CBSE and CISCE usually come in May; UP, Bihar, MP, Maharashtra and Rajasthan follow between April and June. Treat every date here as expected until your board's official notice confirms it.

FAQs

When will the CBSE 10th result 2026 come?

Expected in May 2026 based on past years. CBSE announces the exact date only 1-2 days before — the official notice is the final word.

Which board declares first?

Bihar board is usually among the earliest (March-April). Most others follow in April-June.

Why do sites show different dates?

Because most dates before the official notice are estimates. Any site claiming a 'confirmed' date without a board notice is guessing.

What time of day do results come?

Usually between 10 AM and 3 PM. Boards announce the time in the same notice as the date.

Will the date be the same for 10th and 12th?

Not always — some boards declare them days or weeks apart. Check your class specifically.
