Post Matric Scholarship Renewal Problems and Solutions: 2026 Ki Poori Checklist

Published: 2026-05-19

Desh ke lakho students ke liye Post Matric Scholarship unki padhai poori karne ka sabse bada medium hoti hai. Lekin achanak kya hota hai? Aap naye saal mein renewal karne jaate hain aur portal par koi na koi technical error aa jata hai. Kabhi login nahi hota, toh kabhi pichle saal ke marks upload karne mein dikkat aati hai.

Aksar students renewal ko bahut halke mein lete hain aur sochte hain ki pichle saal toh paisa aa hi gaya tha, is baar toh bas click karna hai. Lekin sach batau toh fresh application se zyada verification rules renewal mein check kiye jaate hain.

Ghar Baithe Sabhi Errors Ka Permanent Solution

1. Previous Application ID / Password Bhool Gaye Hain? +
SOLUTION

Humesha pichle saal ke registered mobile number aur Aadhaar card ka use karke portal ke “Forgot Application ID / Password” option par jayein. Agar portal par server down dikhaye, toh apne college ke scholarship nodal officer se contact karein; unke institute login dashboard me aapki purani ID maujood hoti hai.

2. Course / Year Option Update Nahi Ho Raha Hai? +
SOLUTION

Agar renewal karte waqt aapka next year (Jaise Sem 1 se Sem 3, ya Year 1 se Year 2) show nahi ho raha hai, toh iska matlab aapke college ne abhi tak portal par seats aur master data renew nahi kiya hai. College ke scholarship clerk se kahein ki woh digital portal par institute profile ko update karein.

3. Previous Year Marks / Result Awaited Problem? +
SOLUTION

Agar aapka pichle semester ya saal ka final result abhi tak jaari nahi hua hai aur form ki last date najdik hai, toh aap marksheet column me “Result Awaited” ya provisional marks fill karke form submit kar sakte hain. Baad me portal open hone par correction window ke madhyam se original marksheet upload kar dein.

4. “Aadhaar Card Already Exists” Error Dikhata Hai? +
SOLUTION

Kuch students renewal karne ke badle galti se “Fresh Application” par click kar dete hain, jisse yeh error aati hai. Renewal karne ke liye hamesha Renewal Login window ka hi upyog karein. Agar fir bhi dikkat aaye, toh browser ki cache clear karke login karein.

Problem 1: Previous Application ID Se Login Na Hona

Sabse pehli dikkat jo National Scholarship Portal (NSP) ya state portals par aati hai, wo hai login error. Jab aap apni purani ID aur password daalte hain, toh portal dikhata hai “Invalid Credentials” ya phir aapka account login hi nahi hota.

Solution:

Ab sarkar ne lagbhag sabhi portals par OTR (One-Time Registration) system mandatory kar diya hai. Agar aapne pichle saal fresh apply kiya tha aur is saal renewal karna chahte hain, toh pehle aapko apni purani ID ko naye OTR system se link karna hoga.

  1. Portal ke login page par jayein aur ‘Update OTR’ ya ‘Link Old Application ID’ par click karein.
  2. Apna Aadhaar-linked mobile number verify karein aur naya 14-digit OTR ID generate karein. Uske baad hi aapka renewal dashboard open hoga.

Problem 2: Marks Aur Attendance Ka Criteria Match Na Hona

Renewal scholarship tabhi milti hai jab aap pichle saal dhang se padhai kar rahe ho. Bahut se students ka renewal form isliye reject ho jata hai kyunki unke pichle saal ke marks required percentage se kam hote hain, ya unki attendance 75% se kam hoti hai.

Solution:

Har scheme ka ek fix rule hota hai (jaise CSS mein kam se kam 50% marks hone compulsory hain).

  • Agar aapka pichle saal koi backlog ya ATKT hai, toh jab tak aap use clear karke pass marksheet nahi late, tab tak renewal approve nahi hoga.
  • Form bharte waqt wahi marks dalein jo aapki official marksheet par hain. CGPA ko percentage mein badalte waqt apne university ke official formula ka hi istemal karein.

Problem 3: “Aadhaar Seeding / NPCI Mapping” Ka Fail Hona

Aapne form sahi se bhar diya, college se verify bhi ho gaya, lekin status dikha raha hai “Payment Failed due to Aadhaar Not Seeded”. Ye aaj kal ka sabse bada aur common problem ban chuka hai.

Solution:

Ab koi bhi scholarship ka paisa account number dekh kar nahi bheja jata, balki Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) ke zariye seedhe aapke Aadhaar number par bheja jata hai.

  • Apne najdiki bank branch mein jayein jahan aapka account hai.
  • Wahan ek form bharein jise NPCI Mapping / Aadhaar Seeding Form kehte hain.
  • Bank wale se bol kar apna DBT status active karwayein. Aap online UIDAI ki website par ja kar bhi check kar sakte hain ki aapka bank account Aadhaar se seed hai ya nahi.

Problem 4: Fee Receipt Aur Documents Upload Error

Renewal karte waqt aapko naye academic year ki tuition fee receipt aur naya bonafide certificate upload karna hota hai. Kai baar website par file size ka error aata hai ya document dhundhla (blur) upload ho jata hai.

Problem 5: College Change Hone Par Form Atak Jana

Kuch bache diploma ke baad direct second year engineering mein admission lete hain ya pichle saal ke baad apna college badal lete hain. Aise mein jab wo renewal karne jaate hain, toh unka purana college hi show karta hai.

Solution:

Agar aapne college badal liya hai, toh aap direct renewal nahi kar sakte.

  • Aapko naye portal par ja kar ‘Withdraw Application’ ka option chunna hoga (agar portal ijazat deta hai).
  • Ya phir aapko scholarship department ya helpdesk ko mail likhkar apna institute profile update karwana hoga. Naye college ke verification ke bina aapka renewal valid nahi mana jayega.

FAQs: Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

1. Kya renewal scholarship ke liye har saal naya income certificate lagta hai? NSP par kuch schemes mein har saal naya income certificate lagana padta hai, jabki state portals (jaise e-Kalyan ya MYSY) par ek baar bana certificate 3 saal tak chalta hai. Lekin safe rehne ke liye agar aapka certificate 6 mahine se purana hai, toh naya banwa lena hi behtar hai.

2. Agar mera renewal form ‘Defective’ ho jaye toh kya karein? Pareshan hone ki jarurat nahi hai. Defective ka matlab hai ki aapke form mein koi choti galti hai. Apne dashboard par login karein, check karein ki kya remark mila hai, use thik karein aur dubara submit kar dein.

3. Renewal scholarship ka paisa kab tak account mein aata hai? Fresh application ke मुकाबले renewal ka paisa thoda jaldi aata hai. Jaise hi aapke college aur district officer se verification poora hota hai, uske 2 se 3 mahine ke andar paisa DBT ke zariye bhej diya jata hai.

4. Kya renewal form mein bank account details change kar sakte hain? Haan, kuch portals par renewal ke waqt bank details edit karne ka option milta hai. Lekin agar aapka account Aadhaar se linked hai, toh bank account number badalne se koi farq nahi padta, paisa usi khate mein jayega jo active hoga.

5. Agar main renewal ki last date bhool gaya toh kya naya form bhar sakta hoon? Nahi, agar aapne ek baar fresh apply kar diya hai toh agle saal aap ‘Fresh’ candidate nahi ban sakte. Agar last date nikal gayi toh aapka us saal ka nuksan ho jayega, isliye dates par hamesha nazar rakhein.
