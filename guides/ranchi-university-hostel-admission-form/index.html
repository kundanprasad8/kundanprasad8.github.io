Ranchi University Hostel Admission Form 2026: Fees, Application Process Aur Rules

Published: 2026-07-30

Ranchi University mei har saal Graduation, Post-Graduation aur Ph.D ki padhai karne hazaron students aate hain. Bahar se aane wale students ke liye sabse pehla kaam hota hai ek safe, sasta aur accha hostel dhoondhna.

Agar aapne Ranchi University ke kisi department ya constituent college mein admission liya hai aur aap hostel lena chahte hai, toh yeh guide aapke kafi kaam aayegi. Step-by-Step Application Guide

  1. College Admission: Complete department enrollment in PG/UG course under Ranchi University.
  2. Form Acquisition: Obtain the official Hostel Admission Form from the Dean Students Welfare (DSW) office or constituent college counter.
  3. Department Verification: Get the form verified and signed by your HOD (Head of Department).
  4. Submission: Submit the form along with required documents to the DSW Office / Hostel Warden.
  5. Merit Allotment: Wait for allotment list based on distance from hometown and academic merit.

Required Documents Check-list

  • Duly filled & verified Hostel Application Form
  • RU Department Admission Slip & Fee Receipt
  • Residential Certificate (Aawasiya Praman Patra)
  • Caste Certificate (if claiming fee exemption/reservation)
  • Marksheet of Previous Qualifying Examination
  • Character Certificate from last attended institution
  • Aadhaar Card & 3 Passport-size Photographs

Important Code of Conduct & Rules

  • Curfew Timing: Entry gate closes by 7:30 PM (Girls Hostels) and 8:30 PM (Boys Hostels).
  • Attendance: Night roll-call is mandatory. Uninformed absence for more than 3 days leads to cancellation of seat.
  • Anti-Ragging Compliance: Ragging is strictly illegal under UGC rules; offenders face immediate expulsion and FIR.
  • Guest Policy: Day-scholars and outsiders are strictly forbidden from staying overnight in hostel rooms.
  • Electrical Appliances: High-voltage equipment (heaters, heavy immersion rods) are strictly prohibited.
Note: Hostel seats are allotted for one academic session at a time and must be renewed annually based on satisfactory conduct and attendance.

Ranchi University Hostel Details: Overview

Ranchi University apne students ko rehne ke liye Morabadi campus aur Basic Science block ke paas hostel ki facility deta hai. Chuki students zyada hai aur seats kam, isliye hostel har kisi ko nahi milta. Selection purely merit aur sarkaari reservation rules ke hisab se hota hai.

Ranchi University Ke Paas Kaun-Kaun Se Hostels Hai?

Ranchi University apne boys aur girls students ke liye alag-alag hostels chalata hai jo main campus aur departments ke kafi paas hain:

  • Boys’ Hostels: Morabadi aur nearby campus mein PG Boys Hostels hain jahan PG, UG aur research scholars reh sakte hain.
  • Girls’ Hostels: Morabadi campus mein PG Girls Hostels hain jahan 24-ghante security, CCTV aur lady wardens rehti hain.

Hostel ke har room mein students ko padhne aur rehne ke liye basic cheezein jaise bed, study table, chair, light, fan aur saaf mess area milta hai.

Hostel Allotment Ke Liye Rules

Agar aap Ranchi University Hostel Admission Form bhar rahe hai, toh aapko in conditions ko poora karna hoga:

  1. Regular Student: Applicant ko Ranchi University ke kisi bhi PG department, vocational course ya constituent college ka regular student hona zaroori hai. Private ya distance students ko hostel nahi milta.
  2. Ghar Ki Doori: Local Ranchi shehar ke aas-paas rehne wale students ko hostel nahi diya jata. Jo students Ranchi se kafi door ke district ya state se hai, unhe pehli priority di jaati hai.
  3. Academic Merit: Admission class 12th ya Graduation mein aaye marks ke aadhar par banayi gayi Merit List se hota hai.
  4. Clean Record: Student ke khilaf kisi bhi tarah ki ragging ya illegal mamle ka record nahi hona chahiye.

Form Ke Sath Lagane Wale Zaroori Documents

Hostel form submit karte time in sabhi documents ki photocopies sath mein zaroor lagayein:

  • Ranchi University Admission Slip ya Fee Receipt
  • Head of Department (HOD) dwara signed Bonafide Certificate
  • Pichli Class ki Marksheet (12th / Graduation Marksheet)
  • Caste Certificate (Agar aap SC, ST, BC-I, BC-II category se apply kar rahe hain)
  • Residential Proof / Awasia Praman Patra (Aadhaar Card ya Voter ID)
  • Income Certificate (Aay Praman Patra – Agar discount chahiye)
  • 4 Passport size photo
  • Medical Fitness Certificate (Doctor dwara signed)

Hostel Form Bharne Ka Step-by-Step Process

Ranchi University mein hostel lene ka tarika kafi simple hai:

Step 1: Form Collect Karein

Admission poora hone ke baad DSW Office (Dean Students’ Welfare), Proctor Office ya University ki website (ranchiuniversity.ac.in) se physical form lein.

Step 2: Form Bharein Aur Documents Attach Karein

Form mein apni saari details dhyan se bharein aur saare required documents sath mein attach karein.

Step 3: HOD Se Verify Karwayein

Apne Department ke HOD ya Principal ke paas ja kar form par unka signature aur stamp lagwayein.

Step 4: Form Submit Karein

Verified form ko DSW Office ya hostel warden ke paas deadline se pehle submit kar dein.

Step 5: Merit List Aur Room Allocation

Merit List mein naam aane par bank challan se Hostel Fee jama karein aur receipt dikha kar room ki key lein.

Hostel Mein Rehne Ke Main Rules

  • Ragging Par Ban: Ragging par poori tarah ban hai. Form ke sath student aur parents dono ko Anti-Ragging Affidavit submit karna hota hai.
  • Curfew Timing: Girls hostel aur Boys hostel dono mein aane-jaane ka fix time hota hai. Late entry ke liye warden se written permission leni hoti hai.
  • Outside Entry: Kisi bhi outsider ya dost ko hostel room mein raat mein rukne ki permission nahi hoti hai.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Ranchi University hostel admission form kab nikalta hai?

Admission poora hone ke theek baad (August se October ke beech) hostel form nikale jaate hain.

2. Kya Ranchi University ke saare students ko hostel mil jata hai?

Nahi, seats limited hain. Hostel sirf unhe milta hai jinki academic merit acchi hoti hai aur jo Ranchi se door se aate hain.

3. Ranchi University hostel ka saalkana kharch kitna aata hai?

Hostel ka saalkana base charge ₹4,500 se ₹6,500 ke beech hota hai. Khane ka kharch lagbhag ₹2,000 se ₹2,500 per month alag se lagta hai.

4. Kya UG aur PG students ke liye alag hostels hain?

Haan, Ranchi University ke paas UG, PG aur research scholars ke liye alag blocks aur rooms allocated hote hain.

5. Allotment hone ke baad room kaise milta hai?

Merit list mein naam aane par bank challan dwara hostel fee jama karein aur fee receipt warden ko dikha kar room possession lein.
