🛕 Ancient Legacy & Unique Traditions

1. 16-Armed Deity (Solha Bhuji): Unlike standard 8 or 10-armed idols, Maa Dewri features a 3-foot tall black stone idol with 16 arms holding traditional weapons, flowers, and sacred objects.

2. Unique Tribal & Brahmin Synthesis: Rituals are performed by Pahans (Munda tribal priests) for 6 days a week, while Brahmin Pandas conduct special pujas 1 day a week (Tuesdays).

3. Interlocking Architecture: Built around the 10th-12th century AD by Munda King Kera of Singhbhum or Nagvanshi rulers using interlocking stone blocks without cement mortar.

4. Sacred Thread Ritual (Mannat): Devotees tie red and yellow sacred threads on bamboo structures to make wishes and return to untie them when granted.

Ancient History & Significance of Dewri Mandir Tamar

Dewri Mandir ka itihas behad rochak aur chamatkari mana jata hai. Temple se judi do mukhya aitihasik (historical) kathaayein famous hain:

1. 16-Armed Ma Durga Idol & Uniques Architecture

Temple me sthapit Ma Durga ki idol ki 16 bhujaayein (arms) hain, jo aamtor par dikhne wali 8 ya 10-armed idols se alag hain. Murti me Goddess Durga ke sath Lord Shiva, Goddess Lakshmi, Goddess Saraswati, Lord Ganesha, aur Lord Kartikeya ki carved images bhi hain.

Temple ki sabse badi khasiyat iska nirman hai—yeh poora temple bina kisi cement ya mortar ke, sirf interlocking stone blocks ko jod kar banaya gaya hai.

2. Tribal & Pahahan Pujari Tradition

Dewri Temple ki ek anokhi parampara hai. Yahan hafte me 6 din Pahahan (Tribal Priests) dwara puja ki jaati hai aur 1 din (Tuesday) Brahmin priests dwara rituals perform kiye jaate hain. Yeh temple Tribal Culture aur Hindu traditions ke ekta ka sabse bada prateek hai.

3. Kera King & Pandava Legend

Local legends ke anusar, 14th century me Kera ke King Rja Baera Sal ne ek battle se wapas laut te waqt is mandir ka re-construction karwaya tha. Ek doosri mythical kahani ke mutabiq, Mahabharata kaal me Pandavon ne apne Agyatvas (exile) ke dauran yahan thahar kar Ma Durga ki aradhna ki thi.

FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

1. Ranchi se Dewri Mandir Tamar ki exact distance kitni hai?

Ranchi Railway Station ya Main Road se Dewri Mandir ki distance lagbhag 60 km se 62 km hai.

2. Dewri Mandir Tamar me murti ki kya khasiyat hai?

Dewri Mandir me Goddess Durga ki 16 bhujaon (16-armed) wali rare stone idol sthapit hai.

3. Dewri Mandir khulne aur band hone ka timing kya hai?

Dewri Mandir daily subah 05:00 AM se shaam 08:00 PM tak darshan ke liye khula rehta hai.

4. Kya Dewri Mandir me tribal pujari puja karwate hain?

Haan, Dewri Mandir me hafte ke 6 din Tribal (Pahan) priests puja karate hain aur Tuesday ko Brahmin priests dwara puja hoti hai.

5. Why is the priesthood system at Dewri Mandir unique?

Dewri Mandir is one of the few temples in India where rituals are jointly conducted by both tribal priests (Pahans) and traditional Brahmin priests. By traditional custom, tribal Pahans perform the primary rituals for six days a week, while Brahmin priests perform the rituals on Sundays.

6. What are the official rates for Vehicle Pooja at the temple?

If you purchase a new vehicle and wish to perform a ritual blessing at the temple, the managed rates are typically:

  • 2-Wheelers & 3-Wheelers: ₹100
  • 4-Wheelers (Cars/SUVs): ₹250
  • Heavy Vehicles (6-wheelers and above): ₹500

7. Which tourist places can be visited near Dewri Mandir on the same day?

Since Dewri Mandir sits directly on the Ranchi–Tata Highway (NH-33), you can easily combine your visit with these popular stops:

  • Surya Mandir, Bundu: ~18 km away (en route from Ranchi).
  • Dassam Falls: ~32 km away via Taimara.
  • Jonha Falls: ~44 km away.
