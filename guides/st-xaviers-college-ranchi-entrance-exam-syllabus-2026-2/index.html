St. Xavier’s College Ranchi Entrance Exam Syllabus 2026: Janiye Har Course Ka Pattern Aur Taiyari Ki Strategy

Published: 2026-06-08

Jharkhand ke sabse prestigious aur top-ranked educational institutions mein se ek, St. Xavier’s College (SXC), Ranchi ne naye academic session ke liye Undergraduate regular aur vocational courses mein admission ki process shuru kar di hai. Ranchi University se affiliated is autonomous college ki sabse badi quality ye hai ki yahan ke almost sabhi high-demand courses (jaise BBA, BCA, B.Com, English Hons, etc.) mein direct admission nahi hota. College inke liye apna alag se entrance test conduct karta hai.

Agar aap is saal St. Xavier’s College Ranchi ka admission form bhar rahe hain, toh exam crack karne ke liye aapko St. Xavier’s College Ranchi entrance exam syllabus 2026 aur badle hue selection pattern ki accurate jankari honi chahiye. Is article mein hum aapko stream-wise syllabus aur paper structure poori tarah clear karenge.

Course-Wise Entrance Exam Syllabus 2026

St. Xavier’s College Ranchi mein har department apna alag question paper design karta hai. Niche aap apne chune hue stream ke according detailed syllabus dekh sakte hain:

1. BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)

BBA ke entrance paper mein aapki analytical capability, communication, aur general business mindset ko test kiya jata hai:

  • English Language: English Grammar, Vocabulary, Synonyms & Antonyms, Reading Comprehension.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Class 10th level tak ki Basic Mathematics (Percentage, Profit & Loss, Ratio & Proportion, Average, Time & Work).
  • Logical Reasoning: Data Interpretation, Puzzles, Number Series, Coding-Decoding.
  • General Awareness: Business dunia ki badi khabrein, Current Affairs, aur Indian Economy ke basics.

2. BCA & B.Sc (Computer Application / Information Technology)

Technical aur IT fields mein jaane ke liye aapka mathematical base behtar hona zaroori hai:

  • Core Mathematics: Class 11th aur 12th level ki Maths (Set Theory, Algebra, Probability, Matrices, Determinants, Calculus).
  • Logical Ability & IQ: Mental aptitude aur logical reasoning se jude sawal.
  • Computer Fundamentals: Basic computer hardware/software terminology, operating systems, aur internet ki basic knowledge.

3. B.Com (Accounts / Commerce Honours)

Commerce background ke students ke liye unke academic core par sabse zyada focus rehta hai:

  • Accountancy & Business Studies: Class 11th aur 12th ka pura syllabus (Journal, Ledger, Partnership Accounts, Share Capital, Principles of Management).
  • Commercial Arithmetic: Business calculations aur basic mathematical problems.
  • Economic Affairs: Current financial trends, banking systems, aur Indian business environment updates.

4. B.A. English (Honours)

English Hons ka entrance dusre normal objective tests se kaafi alag hota hai kyunki isme aapki writing depth dekhi jaati hai:

  • Objective Section: English Literature ke basics, famous authors, poets aur unki plays/books ke naam, advanced grammar.
  • Descriptive Section: Isme aapko subjective answers likhne hote hain jaise Essay writing, Creative paragraph compilation, ya kisi passage par apna critical evaluation dena.

Entrance Clear Karne Ka Step-by-Step Plan

St. Xavier’s ka entrance clear karne ke liye aapko ek structured schedule follow karna hoga:

1.11th-12th Ke Textbooks Ko Base Banayein:

Apne stream se jude core subjects ke basic concepts ko NCERT se acche se revise karein.

2.Aptitude Aur GK Par Rozana Waqt Dein:

BBA/BCA ke candidates alag se RS Aggarwal ya kisi standard book se quantitative aptitude aur regular current affairs ki practice karein.

3.Time Management Ka Abhyas Karein:

Exam mein limited time milta hai, isliye ghar par mock questions ko limited time period solve karne ki speed badhayein.

4.Official Web Notifications Monitor Karein:

Exam se ek hafta pehle sxcran.org portal par ja kar naye rules, center layout, aur specific reporting time ko check karna na bhoolein.

FAQs:

1. Kya Arts ka student BBA ke entrance exam mein baith sakta hai?

Haan, St. Xavier’s Ranchi mein BBA course ke liye kisi bhi stream (Arts, Science, Commerce) ke eligible students apply kar sakte hain, basharte unka 12th mein aggregate marks college ke minimum eligibility criteria (aamtaur par 50% se upar) ko match karta ho.

2. Kya BCA entrance ke liye 12th mein Maths hona compulsory hai?

Haan, college ke niyam ke mutabik BCA aur B.Sc Computer Application/IT jaise professional courses ke liye intermediate (12th) level par Mathematics ya Business Mathematics ka hona behad zaroori hai.

3. Entrance test clear karne ke baad kya interview bhi hota hai?

BBA aur kuch special vocational courses mein written entrance pass karne ke baad short-listed candidates ko Personal Interview (PI) ya Group Discussion (GD) ke liye bulaya jata hai. Regular BA, BSc, aur BCom mein aamtaur par direct entrance merit ke basis par selection ho jata hai.

4. Written exam ke marks ke alawa kya 12th board ke marks bhi final merit mein judte hain?

Haan, final selection list taiyar karte waqt college ek specific weightage formula lagata hai. Isme aapke entrance test score aur 12th board ke marks dono ko mila kar ek combined merit index banaya jata hai.

5. SXC Ranchi Entrance Exam ka admit card kab aur kahan milta hai?

Application process poori hone ke lagbhag 5 se 7 dinon ke andar college ki official website sxcran.org par admit card ka link active kiya jata hai. Aap apne login credentials dal kar use online download kar sakte hain.
