CUET UG 2026 Answer Key Release Date Aur Challenge Process: Step-by-Step Poori Jankari

Published: 2026-06-08

National Testing Agency (NTA) dwara conduct kiya jane wala Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG 2026) khatam hone ke baad ab lakho bacho ko sirf ek hi cheez ka intezar hai unka raw score. Desh ki top central universities (jaise DU, BHU, JNU, JMI) aur state universities (jaise Ranchi University) mein admission isi score ke basis par milna hai.

Result se theek pehle NTA aapki Provisional Answer Key aur Response Sheet (jo aapne exam mein marks tick kiye the) jari karta hai. Isse aapko result se pehle hi andaza ho jata hai ki aapka score kitna aane wala hai. Lekin bahut baar NTA ki answer key mein kuch questions ke answers galat hote hain, jise thik karwane ke liye aapko use “Challenge” karna padta hai.

CUET UG 2026 Answer Key: Important Dates

NTA ke pichle kuch saalo ke trend ke acording, exams khatam hone ke around 10 se 15 dino ke andar provisional answer key unke official portal par live kar di jati hai.

  • CUET UG 2026 Exams End: May 2026 ke aakhri hafte tak saare phase ke exams complete hone ki umeed hai.
  • Provisional Answer Key Release Date: June 2026 ke dusre hafte (Expected 10th to 15th June) tak answer key live ho jayegi.
  • Objection Window Duration: Answer key release hone ke baad NTA bacho ko challenge karne ke liye sirf 2 se 3 din ka samay deta hai.
  • Final Answer Key & Result: June 2026 ke aakhri hafte tak final key aur result dono declare kar diye jaate hain.

CUET UG Answer Key Aur Response Sheet Kaise Download Karein?

Apna score match karne ke liye aapko niche diye gaye tarike se login karna hoga:

  1. Sabse pehle CUET UG ki official website exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG par jayein.
  2. Homepage par “CUET UG 2026 Answer Key & Response Sheet Login” ke link par click karein.
  3. Apna Application Number aur Date of Birth (ya Password) enter karke login karein.
  4. Dashboard par aapko do alag links milenge: ek aapki personal Response Sheet (jo aap exam hall mein mark karke aaye hain) aur dusra Provisional Answer Key. Dono ko download kar lein.

Step-by-Step: NTA CUET Answer Key Ko Challenge Kaise Karein?

Agar aapko lagta hai ki aapka jawab sahi hai aur NTA ne use galat diya hai, toh aap is step-by-step process se online objection file kar sakte hain:

1.Login Aur ‘Challenge’ Option Par Jayein:

Apne CUET dashboard par login karne ke baad, wahan diye gaye “View/Challenge Answer Key” ke link par click karein.

2.Question ID Aur Correct Option Select Karein:

Aapke samne ek bada table khulega jahan har Question ki ek unique ‘Question ID’ hogi aur uske samne NTA ka sahi ‘Correct Option ID’ likha hoga. Agar aapko lagta hai koi dusra option sahi hai, toh uske samne diye gaye check-box par tick kar dein.

3.Supporting Documents Upload Karein:

Sirf tick karne se NTA aapki baat nahi manega. Aapko ek single PDF file bana kar upload karni hogi jisme aapka solution ho ya kisi standard NCERT textbook ka screenshot/photo ho jo aapke answer ko sahi proof karta ho. Ise ‘Choose File’ par click karke upload karein.

4.Claims Ko Save Karein:

Apne saare selected questions ko re-check karne ke baad niche diye gaye “Save your Claims and Pay Fee” par click karein.

5.Challenge Fee Ka Online Payment:

NTA har ek question ko challenge karne ke liye ₹200 ki non-refundable processing fee leta hai. Agar aap 2 questions challenge kar rahe hain toh ₹400 pay karne honge. Ise Debit Card, Credit Card ya UPI ke zariye online pay kar dein.

Challenge Submit Hone Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai?

Jab objection window band ho jati hai, toh NTA ke subject experts ki ek team sabhi students ke challenges ko check karti hai.

  • Agar aapka challenge sahi paya gaya, toh answer key ko badla jata hai aur uska fayda un sabhi students ko milta hai jinhone wo exam shift di thi chahe unhone challenge kiya ho ya nahi.
  • Iske baad ek Final Answer Key taiyar hoti hai aur usi ke basis par aapka percentile score nikal kar final result announce kiya jata hai. Final key aane ke baad koi bhi naya challenge accept nahi hota.

FAQs:

1. CUET UG 2026 answer key check karne ki official website kya hai?

Iske liye official NTA portal exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG hai. Kisi bhi fake website par apni application details enter na karein.

2. Kya answer key challenge karna sabhi bacho ke liye compulsory hai?

Nahi, bilkul nahi. Agar aap apne score se santusht (satisfied) hain aur aapko lagta hai ki NTA ke saare answers sahi hain, toh aapko challenge karne ki koi zaroorat nahi hai.

3. Agar mere friend ne ek question challenge kar diya hai, toh kya mujhe bhi karna hoga?

Nahi, agar kisi ek student ka bhi challenge accept ho jata hai, toh NTA us question ke marks us shift ke sabhi eligible students ko deta hai. Bar-bar paise kharch karne ki zaroorat nahi hai agar aapka dost pehle hi proof bhej chuka hai.

4. Kya final answer key aane ke baad bhi marks badal sakte hain?

Nahi, final answer key ko aakhri (ultimate) mana jata hai. Uske upar koi objection nahi liya jata aur usi ke marks ke bais par central universities ka admission portal open hota hai.

5. Response Sheet mein ‘Dropped’ question ka kya matlab hota hai?

Agar kisi question mein koi technical error tha ya saare options galat the, toh NTA use ‘Dropped’ mark kar deta hai. Aise questions ke poore marks us shift ke sabhi bacho ko de diye jaate hain.
