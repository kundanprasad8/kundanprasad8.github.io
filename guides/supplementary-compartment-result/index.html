Supplementary & Compartment Result: How to Check

Published: 2026-10-12

Latest Update: Boards declare supplementary/compartment results 4-6 weeks after the supplementary exams. Last Updated On: 13 October 2026

If you did not clear one or two subjects in your board exams, you do not lose the full year. Every major board — CBSE, UPMSP, BSEB, MPBSE and others — holds supplementary (compartment) exams and publishes a separate result for them.

At a Glance: Supplementary exams are a second chance for students who failed in 1-2 subjects. You re-appear only in those subjects, and the new result replaces the old marks for them. Check it on your board's official result portal with your roll number.

Supplementary Result — What Is It?

A supplementary (called "compartment" by CBSE) result is issued after the re-exam for failed subjects. Pass it, and your marksheet is updated — you move to the next class without repeating the year.

How to Check Your Supplementary Result

  1. Open your board's official result portal (the same site as the main result).
  2. Look for the "Supplementary" or "Compartment" result link for your class.
  3. Enter your roll number and date of birth / school code as asked.
  4. Your updated result appears — check the new marks in the re-appeared subjects.
  5. Download and print it; collect the updated marksheet from your school later.

FAQs

How many subjects can I give in supplementary?

Most boards allow 1-2 subjects. CBSE allows one subject under the compartment scheme; many state boards allow two. Check your board's notice for the exact rule.

Will my marksheet show that I gave a supplementary exam?

Your updated marksheet shows the new marks. Some boards add a small remark; it does not affect college admission as long as you pass.

What if I fail the supplementary exam too?

Then you repeat the class or appear as a private candidate next year, depending on your board's rules. Talk to your school about the best option.

Is the supplementary syllabus the same?

Yes — you study the same syllabus, only for the subjects you re-appear in.

When do supplementary results come out?

Roughly 4-6 weeks after the supplementary exams, which are usually held 1-2 months after the main result. Exact dates come in your board's notice.

Do I need to fill a form for the supplementary exam?

Yes. Your school usually fills it, or you apply online on the board portal before the deadline. There is a per-subject fee.

Do not treat a supplementary exam as a failure — treat it as a second attempt. Thousands of students clear it every year and continue without losing time.
