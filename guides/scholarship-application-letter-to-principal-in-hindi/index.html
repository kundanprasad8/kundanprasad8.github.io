Scholarship Application Letter to Principal in Hindi: Fees Maafi Aur Scholarship Ke Liye Prarthana Patra Kaise Likhein?

Published: 2026-05-19

School ho ya college, padhai ka kharcha aaj kal kaafi badh gaya hai. Bahut baar family ka financial condition aisi nahi hoti ki bache poori fees ek saath bhar sakein. Aise mein har school aur college mein smart aur needy bachcho ke liye scholarship ya fees maafi ki suvidha hoti hai. Lekin iska fayda uthane ke liye aapko apne Principal ko ek proper application likhni padti hai.

Bahut se bache aur parents is baat ko lekar pareshan rehte hain ki hindi mein application ka sahi format kya hoga. Agar prarthana patra mein sahi sabdo aur tarike ka istemal na kiya jaye, toh Principal sir use reject bhi kar sakte hain.

Main pichle saal ek sarkari school mein ek student ki help kar raha tha, jiske pitaji ki job chali gayi thi. Humne milkar ek behtar format mein prarthana patra likha aur Principal sir ne uski 80% fees maaf kar di. Isliye aaj main aapko batane wala hoon ki scholarship application letter to principal in Hindi kaise likhein jo ek baar mein approve ho jaye.

Application Likhte Waqt In Zaruri Baaton Ka Dhyaan Rakhein

Principal ko patra likhna ek formal kaam hai, isliye iska system samajhna bahut jaruri hai:

  • Sahi Format: Hindi application mein ‘Seva Mein’, ‘Vishay’ (Subject), aur ‘Mahodaya/Mahoday’ ka sahi jagah par hona bahut jaruri hai.
  • Aasan Bhasha: Bookish ya bohot kathin words ka use na karein. Apni baat ko simple aur saaf sabdo mein likhein.
  • Reason Clear Karein: Aapko scholarship kyun chahiye (jaise pitaji ki kam aay, ya ghar mein koi medical emergency), ise saaf-saaf batayein.
  • Academic Record: Agar aap padhai mein acche hain (jaise pichli class mein 80% marks aaye hain), toh use application mein jarur mention karein. Isse impact achha padta hai.

School Students Ke Liye Scholarship Application Format

Agar aap school ke student hain ya apne bache ke liye application likhna chahte hain, toh is sample format ka use kar sakte hain:

Seva Mein, Pradhanacharya Mahoday, [Aapke School Ka Naam] [Shahar Ka Naam, Jaise- Ranchi]

Vishay: Scholarship (Chhatravriti) aur Fees Maafi ke liye prarthana patra.

Mahoday,

Saviney nivedan ye hai ki main aapke school mein Kaksha [Aapki Class, Jaise- 9th] ka chhatra hoon. Main pichle kai saalo se aapke school mein padh raha hoon aur har saal meri class mein acche marks aate hain. Padhai ke saath-saath main school ki khel-kood pratiyogitaon mein bhi bhaag leta raha hoon.

Mere pitaji ek chote kisan/mazdoor hain aur unki varshik aay bahut kam hai. Ghar mein 4 sadasya hain, jiske karan ghar ka kharcha chalana hi mushkil ho jata hai. Aise sthiti mein mere pitaji meri aage ki padhai ki fees bharne mein asamarth hain. Main apni padhai aage jari rakhna chahta hoon kyunki mera sapna ek engineer/doctor banne ka hai.

Atah aap se namra nivedan hai ki meri parivarik sthiti ko dekhte hue mujhe is saal ki school scholarship dene ki kripa karein, taaki meri padhai na ruke. Iske liye main aapka hamesha aabhari rahunga.

Aapka Aagyakari Shishya Naam: [Aapka Naam] Kaksha: [Class aur Section] Roll Number: [Aapka Roll No] Date: [Aaj Ki Date]

College/University Students Ke Liye Scholarship Application Format

College mein thoda professional format chalta hai. Agar aap B.A, B.Sc, B.Tech ya kisi aur course ke university student hain, toh is format ka use karein:

Seva Mein, Pradhanacharya / Dean Mahoday, [College/University Ka Naam] [Ranchi, Jharkhand]

Subject: College Scholarship Scheme ke tahat financial help ke liye aavedan.

Mahodaya,

Main [Aapka Naam], aapke prathisthit college mein [Course Ka Naam, Jaise- B.Tech 2nd Year] ka chhatra hoon. Pichele semester mein maine [Aapke Marks/CGPA, Jaise- 8.5 CGPA] hasil kiye hain aur mera academic record hamesha se accha raha hai.

Main aapko batana chahta hoon ki mere parivar ki arthik sthiti filhaal thik nahi hai. Mere pitaji ghar ke akele kamane wale hain aur unki aay se ghar ka rashan aur dawaiyo ka kharcha hi mushkil se nikal pata hai. College ki naye saal ki fees bharna hamare liye bahut mushkil ho gaya hai.

Maine suna hai ki hamare college mein deserving students ke liye ‘Student Welfare Scholarship’ di jati hai. Main is scholarship ke liye sabhi criteria ko poora karta hoon. Agar mujhe ye scholarship milti hai, toh main bina kisi tanaav ke apni padhai par focus kar paunga.

Maine is application ke saath apni pichli marksheet aur pitaji ka Income Certificate (Aay Praman Patra) attach kar diya hai. Kripa karke mere aavedan par vichar karein.

Aapka Vishwasi Naam: [Aapka Naam] Enrollment Number: [Aapka Number] Department: [Aapka Subject] Mobile Number: [Aapka Phone No]

FAQs: Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

1. Kya application khud ke haath se (handwritten) likhna jaruri hai? School mein hamesha ek plain white paper par haath se likhi hui (handwritten) application hi sabse best mani jati hai. College mein aap chahein toh ise type karke printout bhi nikaal sakte hain.

2. Application mein vishay (Subject) likhna kyun jaruri hai? Subject dekh kar hi Principal ko ek baar mein samajh aa jata hai ki patra kis baare mein hai. Agar aap vishay nahi likhenge, toh aapka patra adhoora mana jayega.

3. Kya is application se e-Kalyan ya NSP scholarship bhi mil sakti hai? Nahi, ye application sirf aapke school ya college level par jo fund hota hai, usse help pane ke liye hai. Sarkari portals (jaise e-Kalyan) ke liye aapko online form bharna padta hai.

4. Agar principal application reject kar dein toh kya karein? Agar Principal sir reject karte hain, toh aap school/college ke ‘Student Welfare Committee’ ya scholarship department se milkar apni problem unhein personal level par samjha sakte hain.

5. Application ke saath kaun se documents lagane chahiye? Aapko apni pichli class ki marksheet, parivar ka Income Certificate, aur agar aapke pass BPL (Below Poverty Line) card ya ration card hai, toh uski copy lagani chahiye.
