Bihar Vidhan Sabha Vacancy 2026: Naye Notification Ki Details Aur PDF Download Kaise Karein?

Published: 2026-05-24

Bihar ke sarkari department mein naukri ki talash kar rahe graduates aur intermediate pass candidates ke liye ek behtareen mauka samne aa raha hai. Bihar Vidhan Sabha (Patna Assembly Secretariat) ne alag-alag group-level posts par bharti ke liye new rules aur recruitment process ko lekar sanket diye hain. Agar aap pichle kuch dino se bihar vidhan sabha vacancy 2026 notification pdf download karne ka sahi link khoj rahe hain, toh yeh article aapke liye hi hai.

Bihar Vidhan Sabha Sachivalaya ke antargat aane wali in bhartiyon mein Security Guard, Data Entry Operator, Driver, aur Office Attendant jaise aakarshak posts include hote hain. Chaliye is saal aane wali vacancies, unki eligibility, aur notification download karne ka sahi tarika samajhte hain.

Bihar Vidhan Sabha Recruitment 2026 Overview

Is recruitment drive se judi important baatein neeche diye gaye table mein dekhi ja sakti hain:

Post-Wise Eligibility Criteria Aur Salary Status

Bihar Vidhan Sabha mein alag-alag posts ke liye educational qualification aur age limit alag-alag decide ki jaati hai:

1. Data Entry Operator

  • Qualification: 12th (Intermediate) pass hone ke sath-sath computer par Hindi aur English typing ka gyan hona zaroori hai (Aamtaur par 8000 key depressions prati ghanta).
  • Salary Scale: Level-4 (₹25,500 – ₹81,100) plus allowances.

2. Security Guard

  • Qualification: Kisi bhi recognized board se 12th pass hona anivarya hai. Isme physical fitness (height aur chest) ke kade niyam hote hain.
  • Salary Scale: Level-3 (₹21,700 – ₹69,100).

3. Office Attendant

  • Qualification: Agar aap sirf 10th (Matric) pass hain, toh aap is post ke liye eligible hain. Isme aamtaur par direct interview ya ek choti screening test ke aadhar par selection hota hai.
  • Salary Scale: Level-1 (₹18,000 – ₹56,900).

Step-by-Step Guide: Notification PDF Kaise Download Karein?

Jab bhi official server par naya recruitment notification release kiya jata hai, toh aap neeche diye gaye steps ke mutabik asli rule-book download kar sakte hain:

1.Vidhan Sabha Ki Website Open Karein:

Sabse pehle apne browser mein Bihar Assembly ki official website biharvidhansabha.bih.nic.in par jayein.

2.Recruitment Tab Par Click Karein:

Homepage par dikh rahe menu bar mein “Recruitment” ya “Notices” wale option ko select karein.

3.Naya Advertisement Dhundhein:

Wahan chal rahe naye updates mein “Internet Advertisement No. 01/2026 – Apply Online for Various Posts” ke link par click karein.

4.PDF File Download Aur Save Karein:

“Download Detailed Advertisement” par click karte hi poora Notification PDF aapke device mein download ho jayega. Form bharne se pehle use dhyan se padhein.

Online Form Kaise Bharein?

Notification download karne ke baad, wahan diye gaye “Apply Online” dashboard par ja kar aapko niche likha tarika follow karna hoga:

  1. Sabse pehle apni basic details (Naam, Mobile Number, Email ID) daal kar New Registration karein.
  2. Registration number milne ke baad login karein aur apni Academic Details fill karein.
  3. Apni passport-size photo aur signature ko bataye gaye resolution mein scan karke upload karein.
  4. Net Banking, Credit Card, ya UPI ke madhyam se application fee jama karein aur final confirmation page ka printout nikal lein.

FAQs: Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

1. Bihar Vidhan Sabha vacancy ke liye minimum age limit kya hai?

Aamtaur par general category ke candidates ke liye minimum aayu 18 saal aur maximum aayu 37 saal hoti hai. Bihar ke niwasi OBC aur Female candidates ko 3 saal aur SC/ST ko 5 saal ki chhoot milti hai.

2. Kya Security Guard post ke liye koi physical exam bhi hota hai?

Haan, Security Guard (Suraksha Prahari) post ke liye 100 marks ki ek daud (Physical Endurance Test) hoti hai, jisme jitna kam samay aap lenge, utne zyada marks milenge. Final merit physical ke marks par hi banti hai.

3. Kya hum ek se zyada posts ke liye apply kar sakte hain?

Haan, agar aap eligible hain toh alag-alag advertisements ke mutabik alag-alag fee dekar ek se zyada posts ke liye aavedan kar sakte hain.
