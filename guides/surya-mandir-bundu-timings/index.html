Surya Mandir Bundu Timings & Travel Guide 2026: Check Nearest Railway Station, Route & Entry Details

Published: 2026-08-07

Ranchi-Jamshedpur Highway (NH-33) par Edalhatu village mei located Surya Mandir Jharkhand ke sabse khoobsurat aur revered spiritual landmarks mei se ek hai. Chariot-shaped architecture me bane is mandir mei 18 vishalkay pahiye aur 7 daudte hue ghode bane hai, jo ise Odisha ke Konark Sun Temple ki jhalak dete hai.

Ranchi city ya nearby districts se picnic aur darshan ka plan bana rahe devotees ke liye Surya Mandir Bundu Timings and Nearest Railway Station ki accurate information hona behad zaruri hai.

🌅 Daily Temple Schedule

  • Temple Opening: 6:00 AM
  • Sunrise Prayer: 6:15 AM – 6:45 AM
  • General Darshan: 6:00 AM – 7:30 PM
  • Evening Aarti: 6:30 PM – 7:00 PM
  • Closing Time: 7:30 PM

💡 Best Visiting Hours

• Early Morning (6:00 AM – 8:00 AM): Ideal for witnessing the sunrise rays hitting the temple front and enjoying peaceful photography.
• Chhath Puja Special: Huge celebrations during Kartik & Chaitra Chhath with morning/evening Arghya at the temple pond.
• Sunday Rush: Sundays attract travelers on the highway; expect light crowds.

🚉 Nearest Railway Stations & Airport

1. Ranchi Junction (RNC) — 39 km: Main railhead connected to all major Indian cities. Taxis, cabs, and buses to Bundu run directly from Ranchi city.

2. Hatia Railway Station (HTE) — 42 km: Key rail station for long-distance trains terminating in Ranchi.

3. Tatanagar Junction (TATA) — 86 km: Ideal railway station for travelers approaching from Jamshedpur/West Bengal.

4. Birsa Munda Airport Ranchi (IXR) — 37 km: Nearest commercial airport with direct flight access.

🛕 The Iconic Chariot Architecture

• 18 Wheels & 7 Horses: Built in the shape of a grand celestial chariot (*Ratha*), the temple features 18 detailed stone-carved wheels pulled by 7 lifelike galloping horses representing the days of the week and sun rays.

• Created By: Built under the guidance of Sanskriti Vihar, led by Ramraksha Shamra, giving Ranchi a signature spiritual and architectural marvel.

• Pond & Chhath Ghat: A sacred water tank within the temple complex serves as a primary site for Chhath Puja rituals in the Ranchi-Bundu belt.

💡 Highway Circuit: Combine Surya Mandir with Dassam Falls (15 km) and Maa Dewri Mandir Tamar (18 km).

Nearest Railway Stations to Surya Mandir Bundu

Surya Mandir Bundu tak train se aane wale passengers ke liye 3 main railway stations sabse convenient hai:

1. Ranchi Junction (RNC) – Main & Best Connected Station

  • Distance: Approx 40 km.
  • Connectivity: India ke sabhi major cities (Delhi, Kolkata, Patna, Mumbai) se direct trains available hain. Station ke bahar se private taxis, rental cabs, aur Tata-route bus services easily mil jaati hain.

2. Hatia Railway Station (HTE)

  • Distance: Approx 38 km.
  • Connectivity: Southern aur Western India se aane wali trains ke liye Hatia best option hai. Yahan se Ring Road / Namkum hokar direct NH-33 pakda ja sakta hai.

3. Muri Junction (MURI)

  • Distance: Approx 42 km.
  • Connectivity: Howrah-Ranchi route par chalne wali express trains Muri par halt karti hain. Muri se Bundu ke liye local autos aur private cars available rehti hai.

How to Reach Surya Mandir Bundu from Ranchi

1. By Road (Car, Cab, or Bus)

  • Route: Ranchi City ➔ Namkum ➔ Taimara Ghati ➔ Edalhatu (Bundu).
  • Highway: National Highway 33 (NH-33 Ranchi-Tata Highway) ek broad 4-lane smooth road hai.
  • Bus Transport: Kantatoli Bus Stand (Ranchi) se Jamshedpur ya Tata jane wali har local aur express bus aapko Surya Mandir turn (Edalhatu) par drop kar sakti hai.

2. By Air

  • Nearest Airport: Birsa Munda Airport, Ranchi (IXR), jo mandir se lagbhag 42 km door hai. Airport se direct prepaid taxi hire karke 1 ghante me mandir pahuncha ja sakta hai.

FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

1. Surya Mandir Bundu ka opening timing kya hai?

Surya Mandir daily subah 06:00 AM se shaam 07:00 PM tak darshan ke liye khula rehta hai.

2. Surya Mandir Bundu ka sabse nearest railway station kaun sa hai?

Sabse well-connected nearest major railway station Ranchi Junction (RNC) hai, jo lagbhag 40 km ki doori par hai.

3. Kya Ranchi se Bundu Surya Mandir ke liye direct bus milti hai?

Haan, Ranchi ke Kantatoli bus stand se Jamshedpur / Tata route par chalne wali sabhi buses Surya Mandir gate (NH-33) par stop karti hain.

4. Kya Surya Mandir premises me parking facility available hai?

Haan, mandir complex ke paas spacious car aur bus parking space available hai.

5. Kya Surya Mandir Bundu me Dharamshala ya stay (rehne) ki suvidha hai?

Mandir trust dwara premises ke paas ek Chharak Dharamshala aur Rest House chalaaya jata hai, jahan nominal charges par room mil jaate hain. Iske alawa, behtar lodging ke liye Ranchi city (40 km) me hotels book karna sabse acha option hai.

6. Bundu Surya Mandir me kon sa festival sabse dhoom-dham se manaya jata hai?

Yahan Chhath Puja aur Sankranti / Tusu Mela sabse bade festivals hain. Chhath ke waqt mandir ke paas sthit sacred pond (talab) par hazaron devotees Arghya dene ke liye ikattha hote hain.

7. Ranchi se Surya Mandir tak auto ya taxi ka kitna fare lagta hai?

Ranchi Station ya Main City se private cab (round trip) ka fare ₹1,500 se ₹2,000 ke beech hota hai. Agar aap budget traveler hain, toh Kantatoli se local bus (₹60–₹80 per person) sabse economical option hai.

8. Kya Surya Mandir me photography aur videography allowed hai?

Mandir ke outer premises, garden area, aur chariot architecture ke paas photography allowed hai. Halanki, main garbhagriha (sanctum sanctorum) ke andar photos lena restricted ho sakta hai, isliye local guidelines zarur follow karein.
