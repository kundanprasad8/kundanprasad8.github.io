Jharkhand Krishi Rin Mafi Yojna Beneficiary List 2026: Name Online Check Karein

Published: 2026-08-20

Jharkhand government ke dwara rajya ke kisano ko badi rahat dete hue Jharkhand Krishi Rin Mafi Yojana (JKRMY) ke tehat Kisan Credit Card (KCC) holders ke crop loan ko maaf kiya ja raha hai. Sarkar ne is yojana ke under eligible beneficiaries ki nai Jharkhand Krishi Rin Mafi Yojna Beneficiary List official portal par publish kar di hai.

🌐 How to Check Beneficiary List Online (Step-by-Step)

  1. Official Website: Go to the official Jharkhand Krishi Rin Mafi Portal at jkrmy.jharkhand.gov.in.
  2. Click Beneficiary Search: Select the ‘Beneficiary Search’ / ‘लाभार्थी सूची देखें’ option from the menu bar.
  3. Select District & Block: Choose your District, Block, Village/Panchayat, and Bank Name.
  4. Enter Details: Input your 12-digit Aadhaar Number or KCC Account Number and click Search.
  5. Check Token & Status: View your name, loan clearance status, and token approval receipt.

📄 Essential Documents for e-KYC & Waiver Approval

  • Aadhaar Card: Must be linked with your active mobile number and KCC bank account.
  • Ration Card: Jharkhand Ration Card (Green/Yellow/Pink) mapping family details.
  • KCC Passbook: Kisan Credit Card account details with loan balance proof.
  • Mobile Number: Registered mobile number for OTP verification.
  • Nominee / Land Documents: Khatiyan / LPC (for Raiyat farmers).

Eligibility Criteria for Crop Loan Waiver

Beneficiary list me naam aane ke liye kisan ko in sharto ko poora karna zaroori hai:

  • Applicant Jharkhand ka permanent resident (mool niwasi) hona chahiye.
  • Kisan ki aayu 18 saal ya usse zyada honi chahiye.
  • Applicant ke paas valid Ration Card (Phool/Gupta/AAY/PHH) hona zaroori hai.
  • Kisan ke paas ek hi active KCC Crop Loan Account hona chahiye.
  • Aavedak kisi rajkiya/sarkari pension ya tax-paying category me nahi hona chahiye.
  • Aadhaar Card aur Bank Account me e-KYC aur DBT active hona compulsory hai.

Required Documents for Verification

Agar aapka naam list me hai lekin payment pending dikha raha hai, toh apne paas ye documents taiyar rakhein:

  1. Aadhaar Card (Mobile Number linked)
  2. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Bank Passbook
  3. Ration Card (Jharkhand State Issued)
  4. Active Mobile Number
  5. Aadhaar Seeded Bank Account Details

Common Problems & Solutions (Rejection / Pending Status)

1. Problem: “Name Missing in Beneficiary List”

  • Reason: Bank dwara aapka data portal par upload na hona ya Aadhaar-KCC mismatch hona.
  • Solution: Apne KCC Bank Branch me jayein aur wahan manager se apna KCC data JKRMY portal par update karwane ko kahein.

2. Problem: “e-KYC Pending Status”

  • Reason: Portal par ₹1 tokenization e-KYC complete na hona.
  • Solution: Apne paas ke kisi bhi CSC (Pragya Kendra) par jayein aur Aadhaar biometric fingerprint dekar e-KYC process complete karayein.

3. Problem: “Bank Account Mismatch or DBT Failed”

  • Reason: Bank account me NPCI mapping / Aadhaar Seeding active na hona.
  • Solution: Apne main bank branch me jaakar NPCI Mapping Form submit karein taaki DBT payment directly aapke account me credit ho sake.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Jharkhand Krishi Rin Mafi Yojna Beneficiary List me naam kaise dekhein?

Aap official portal jkrmy.jharkhand.gov.in par jaakar ‘Beneficiary Search’ tab par click karke apne Aadhaar Number ya KCC Account Number se naam check kar sakte hain.

2. Kya JKRMY me ₹1 ka e-KYC karna zaroori hai?

Haan, yojana ka fayda lene aur loan mafi amount approve karne ke liye Pragya Kendra (CSC) se ₹1 ka e-KYC biometric verification zaroori hai.

3. Ration card na hone par kya rin mafi ka laabh milega?

JKRMY ke rules ke mutabiq beneficiary list me shamil hone ke liye applicant ke parivar ka Jharkhand Ration Card database me naam hona zaroori hai.

4. Loan mafi ka paisa kahan credit hota hai?

Rin mafi ki amount kisi kisan ke personal savings account me nahi aati, balki direct aapke KCC Loan Account me transfer karke loan clearance status update kar diya jata hai.
