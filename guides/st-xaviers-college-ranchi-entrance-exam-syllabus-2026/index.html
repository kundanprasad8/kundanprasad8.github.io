St. Xavier’s College Ranchi Entrance Exam Syllabus 2026: Course-Wise Exam Pattern Aur Taiyari Ki Details

Published: 2026-05-26

One of Jharkhand's top colleges, St. Xavier's College, Ranchi opened admissions for the new academic session in undergraduate regular and vocational courses. This autonomous college affiliated with Ranchi University is known for one thing: for most in-demand courses — BBA, BCA, B.Com, English Honours and others — admission is not decided on Class 12 marks alone. The college conducts its own entrance test.

If you filled the St. Xavier's College Ranchi admission form this year, you need accurate information about the St. Xavier's College Ranchi entrance exam syllabus 2026 and exam pattern to clear it. This guide explains the admission process, the entrance test and the paper pattern in simple words.

How Admission Works at St. Xavier's Ranchi

  1. Apply online on the college's official website (sxcran.org) — one-time registration covers multiple courses, with the application fee paid separately for each form.
  2. Appear for the entrance test (XET) — the Xavier Entrance Test — for courses that require it. Some courses admit on Class 12 merit instead.
  3. Check the merit list published on the official website.
  4. Confirm admission on the college admission portal (onlineforms.sxcran.org) — upload documents for verification and pay the fee.

What the Entrance Test Usually Covers

The exact course-wise syllabus comes out in the college's admission notification each year — treat that as final. The typical pattern for XET is objective-type questions from English language, general knowledge and current affairs, logical reasoning, and subject aptitude for the course you chose (for example, basic mathematics and computer awareness for BCA, commerce awareness for B.Com).

Documents Usually Needed

  • Class 10 certificate showing name, father's name and date of birth
  • School leaving certificate and migration certificate (original)
  • Character certificate (original)
  • Class 12 marksheet and admit card
  • Recent passport-size and stamp-size photographs
  • Caste and income certificates (for ST/SC/BC-I/BC-II quota)
  • Baptism certificate (for Christian quota, if applicable)
  • Anti-ragging declaration and undertaking

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an entrance test for every course?

No. The entrance test applies to specific courses — many others admit on Class 12 merit. The admission notification lists which courses need the test.

What is XET?

The Xavier Entrance Test — the college's own entrance examination for the courses that require a test score for admission.

Where is the merit list published?

On the college's official website, sxcran.org. Selected candidates then complete admission formalities on onlineforms.sxcran.org.

Can I apply for more than one course?

Yes — a single registration lets you apply for multiple courses, but the application fee is charged separately for each form.
