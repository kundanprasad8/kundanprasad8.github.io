Board Toppers List: How Merit Lists Are Made

Published: 2026-10-12

Latest Update: Most boards release topper/merit lists together with the result, or within a few days. Last Updated On: 13 October 2026

Every result season, the board toppers list — the students with the highest marks — becomes the most searched page after the result itself. Here is how these lists are actually made and where to find the real ones.

At a Glance: A topper list ranks students by total marks (or percentage) in the board exam. Boards publish it officially; many "topper" pages on random sites are copied or incomplete. Always check the board's own press note or result portal.

Topper List — What Is It?

A merit/toppers list names the highest-scoring students in a board exam — usually the top 10 ranks, sometimes district-wise toppers too.

Quick Info

PointDetail
Ranking basisTotal marks / aggregate percentage
Tie-breakersBoards use subject-wise marks, then age (older student ranked higher in some boards)
Released byThe board, with or just after the result
CBSE noteCBSE stopped publishing an official merit list; percentages are on individual marksheets

How to Find the Official Topper List

  1. Open your board's official website after the result is declared.
  2. Look for the result press note / press release PDF — toppers are usually named there.
  3. District-wise lists appear on some boards' portals (e.g., UPMSP, BSEB).
  4. Ignore forwarded WhatsApp images — verify names against the board's own document.

FAQs

Does CBSE release a toppers list?

No. CBSE discontinued official merit lists. Your marksheet shows your own percentage; school-level toppers are announced by schools themselves.

How are ties broken in topper lists?

Boards compare marks in specific subjects (often the language or core subjects), and some use date of birth as the final tie-breaker. The exact rule is in each board's regulations.

Do toppers get any prize?

Many state governments give cash awards, laptops or scholarships to state and district toppers. Check your state's education department announcements.

Can I get the full merit list?

Boards usually publish only the top ranks publicly. Full merit data is not released for privacy reasons.

Why do different sites show different toppers?

Because many sites copy from each other and add errors. The board's press note is the only source to trust.

Being on the list is an honour, but it is one exam on one day. What you do with the next four years matters far more than your rank on this list.
