🎫 VIP Pass & Arghya System Entry Rules

• Arghya System: Direct entry inside the core garbha griha is restricted on Mondays to manage crowds. Jalabhishek is performed using external copper pipes/vessels (Arghya system).
• VIP Pass / Senior Citizen Entry: Special passes issued by Pahari Mandir Vikas Samiti for elderly devotees, divyangjan, and VIPs with designated time slots near the main stairs.

Sawan Somvari Special Jalabhishek & Arghya System

Sawan ki har Somvari (Monday) ko Lakhon Kanwariya Suvarnarekha River ya Pahari Mandir ke niche sthit pond se jal lekar 468 stairs chadhkar hilltop pahunchte hain.

1. Arghya System for Direct Water Offering

Bheed ko control karne aur sabhi devotees ko jal arpan ka mauka dene ke liye main Garbhagriha ke bahar Arghya System (Pipes/Panchamrit Arghya) install kiya jata hai. Devotees bahar se hi pipe-funnel ke jariye Shivling par Jal aur Milk offer kar sakte hain.

2. Separate Entry & Exit Stairs

  • Upward Route (Entry): Main stairs route se devotees ko hilltop par jane ki permission milti hai.
  • Downward Route (Exit): Dedicated exit stairs route se devotees ko niche bheja jata hai taaki stairs par koi stampede ya jam jaisa mahol na bane.

Facilities & Guidelines for Devotees in Sawan

  • Free Shoe Stand & Luggage Counter: Mandir ke foot-hills par Prasad stalls aur Samiti dwara free shoe stand & luggage counter operate kiye jaate hain.
  • Medical Camps & Water Booths: Hilltop aur stairs ke beech-beech me ORS, drinking water booths, aur district administration ke medical relief camps setup rehte hain.
  • CCTV & Security: Security forces aur volunteer teams pooray temple premises me 24/7 monitor karti hain.

How to Reach Pahari Mandir Ranchi

  • From Ranchi Railway Station (RNC): Distance lagbhag 7 km hai. Station se Auto, E-Rickshaw ya Cab lekar aap 20-25 minutes me Ratu Road / Pahari Mandir gate pahunch sakte hain.
  • From Khadgara / Kantatoli Bus Stand: Distance lagbhag 6.5 km hai.
  • Parking Zone: Sawan Somvari ke din Pahari Mandir road par vehicle entry restricted rehti hai. Cars aur bikes ko Ratu Road main road ya dedicated parking spots par park karna hota hai.

FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

1. Sawan Somvari ko Pahari Mandir Ranchi kitne baje khulta hai?

Sawan Somvari ke din Pahari Mandir ke kapat subah 3:00 AM hi khol diye jaate hain.

2. Pahari Mandir par Jalabhishek karne ke liye kitni stairs chadhni padti hain?

Pahari Mandir hilltop par Lord Shiva ke darshan ke liye lagbhag 468 stairs (seedyon) ka safar tay karna hota hai.

3. Kya Sawan me Pahari Mandir par Senior Citizens ke liye koi special arrangement hota hai?

Senior citizens aur Divyang devotees ke liye specific hours me foothill arghya counter ya priority queue facilities provide ki jaati hain.

4. Pahari Mandir Ranchi ki Sandhya Aarti ka timing kya hai?

Sawan ke mahine me Sandhya Aarti shaam ko lagbhag 07:30 PM se 08:30 PM ke beech hoti hai.
