UP Scholarship Suspension Status Kaise Check Karein? Ghar Baithe 2 Minute Mein Janein

Published: 2026-05-19

Uttar Pradesh ke lakho bacho ke liye UP Scholarship unki padhai ka kharcha uthane ka sabse bada sahara hai. Lekin kai baar sab kuch sahi bharne ke baad bhi bacho ke bank account mein paisa nahi aata. Jab wo status check karte hain, toh wahan “Suspended”, “Rejected” ya “Pending” dikha raha hota hai.

Sarkar har saal data verify karne ke liye strict rules chalati hai, jisme thodi si bhi galti hone par form ko suspend kar diya jata hai. Agar aapka form bhi is saal suspend ho gaya hai ya aap apna status check nahi kar pa rahe hain, toh chinta mat kijiye.

UP Scholarship Suspend Kyun Hoti Hai?

Pehle ye samajhna jaruri hai ki Social Welfare Department (Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) aapke form ko suspend kyun karta hai. Iske piche kuch main reasons hote hain:

  • Marks Mismatch: Agar aapne pichle saal ke marks form mein galat bhar diye hain aur university ka data match nahi hua.
  • Attendance Less Than 75%: Agar aapke college ne aapki attendance 75% se kam online forward ki hai.
  • Duplicate Mobile Number: Ek hi mobile number se kai saare parivar ke bacho ka registration hona.
  • Income/Caste Certificate Issue: Agar aapka aay ya jati praman patra online verify nahi ho pata ya expire ho chuka hai.

UP Scholarship Suspension Status Kaise Check Kare? Step by Step Process

UP scholarship ka status aap official portal (scholarship.up.gov.in) par ja kar aasan steps mein check kar sakte hain. Chaliye poora tarika samajhte hain.

Step 1: Official UP Scholarship Portal Par Jayein

Sabse pehle apne mobile ya laptop par UP Scholarship ki official website scholarship.up.gov.in ko open karein.

Step 2: Student Login Section Mein Jayein

Website ke home page par aapko ‘Student’ ka ek option dikhega. Us par click karte hi aapko do options milenge:

  • Fresh Login: Agar aapne is saal naya form bhara hai.
  • Renewal Login: Agar aap agle saalo (2nd, 3rd year) ka form renewal kar rahe hain. Apne course ke hisab se (Prematric, Postmatric Intermediate, ya Postmatric Other Than Inter) par click karein.

Step 3: Apni Details Enter Karein

Ab aapke samne ek login page khulega. Yahan aapko ye details bharni hain:

  1. Aapka Registration Number (Jo form bharte waqt mila tha).
  2. Aapki Date of Birth (DOB).
  3. Aapka Password (Jo aapne form bharte waqt banaya tha).
  4. Screen par dikh raha Captcha Code. Sab kuch bharne ke baad ‘Submit’ par click kar dein.

Step 4: Current Status Par Click Karein

Login hote hi aapka dashboard khul jayega. Left side mein aapko ek green ya blue color ki link dikhegi jisme likha hoga “Check Current Status”. Us par click karte hi aapke samne ek naya page khulega jahan aapka poora status clear dikh jayega.

Suspension Status Alag-Alag Dikha Raha Hai Toh Kya Karein?

Jab aap status dekhenge, toh wahan ‘Scrutiny Result’ mein kuch alag remarks likhe ho sakte hain. Unka matlab aur solution niche dekhein:

  • Suspended by District Scholarship Committee: Iska matlab hai ki district level par aapke documents mein koi galti payi gayi hai. Iske liye aapko turant apne college ke scholarship department mein sampark karna hoga aur sahi documents dubara submit karne honge.
  • Pending at District Scholarship Committee: Iska matlab hai ki aapka form bilkul sahi hai, bas abhi district officer ne use approve nahi kiya hai. Isme aapko kuch nahi karna, bas thoda intezar karna hai.
  • Rejected due to Ineligibility: Agar aapke parivar ki income limit (OBC/General ke liye ₹2 Lakh aur SC/ST ke liye ₹2.5 Lakh) se zyada hai, toh form reject ho jata hai. Iska koi solution nahi hota agar data sach mein galat hai.

Correction Window Ka Use Kaise Karein?

Agar aapka status suspend dikha raha hai aur sarkar ne Correction Window open ki hai, toh aap apne dashboard par login karke galti ko thik kar sakte hain. Form thik karne ke baad uska printout aur sahi document ki photocopy apne college mein dhyan se jama kar dein, tabhi aapka status dubara update hoga.

FAQs: Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

1. UP scholarship ka status check karne ki official website kaun si hai? Iske liye official website scholarship.up.gov.in hai. Iske alawa aap PFMS portal par bhi apna payment status check kar sakte hain.

2. Agar scholarship status “Suspended” dikha raha hai toh kya paisa aayega? Agar status suspended hai, toh jab tak aap use correction window ke zariye ya college se baat karke thik nahi karwate, tab tak aapka paisa bank account mein nahi aayega.

3. UP scholarship mein attendance kitni honi chahiye? UP government ke rules ke mutabik scholarship pane ke liye aapki college mein kam se kam 75% attendance honi compulsory hai. Agar isse kam hui toh form automatically suspend ho jata hai.

4. Kya password bhool jaane par status check kar sakte hain? Haan, login page par aapko ‘Forgot Password’ ka option milta hai. Wahan apna 10th ka roll number, passing year aur registration number daal kar aap naya password generate kar sakte hain.

5. College se form forward hua hai ya nahi, kaise pata karein? Jab aap apna status check karenge, toh wahan ‘Status at Institution Level’ likha hota hai. Agar wahan ‘Forwarded’ likha hai, toh iska matlab college ne aapka form aage bhej diya hai.
