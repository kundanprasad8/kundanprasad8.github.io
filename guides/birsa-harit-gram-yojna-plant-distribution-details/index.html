Birsa Harit Gram Yojna Plant Distribution Details 2026: Fruit Trees, Slabs & Apply

Published: 2026-08-20

Jharkhand Rural Development Department dwara chalyi ja rahi Birsa Harit Gram Yojana ke under MGNREGA ke through se state ke chhote, seemant aur garib kisano ko banjar zameen par fruit plantation lagwane ke liye free saplings aur majdoori di jaati hai. Is yojana ka main goal kisano ki income badhana, mitti ke koto ko rokna aur environment ki protection karna hai.

Total Saplings 112 Plants

Key Incentive: 100% free saplings provided along with bio-fencing support, pit digging labor wages paid via MGNREGA Job Card, and maintenance wages for 3 consecutive years.

🥭 Approved Fruit Saplings & Timber Boundary Plants

Mango (मुख्य फल):
Amrapali, Mallika, Himsagar, Langra (High density planting models).
Guava & Citrus (अंतर-फसल):
L-49 (Lucknow 49), Allahabad Safeda, Kagzi Lemon, Sweet Lime.
Jackfruit & Papaya:
Singapore hybrid jackfruit, Red Lady Papaya for quick yearly yield.
Boundary Timber (इमारती लकड़ी):
Mahogany, Teak (सागवान), Neem along orchard boundaries for wind protection.

📋 Application Procedure for Farmers & Landowners

  1. Eligibility Check: Small/marginal farmers, ST/SC families, women-headed households, or families with active MGNREGA Job Cards owning unutilized/tanr land.
  2. Gram Sabha Approval: Contact your local Gram Panchayat / Rozgar Sevak to get land approval registered during the Gram Sabha planning meet.
  3. Document Submission: Submit Aadhaar Card, MGNREGA Job Card, Land Rights Certificate (Khatian/LPC), Bank Passbook, and Passport photos to the Panchayat Sevak/Block Development Office (BDO).
  4. Pit Digging & Sapling Delivery: Upon approval, MGNREGA scheme code is generated. Pit digging labor starts in May–June, followed by free sapling distribution before monsoon rains.

Key Highlights of Birsa Harit Gram Scheme 2026

Birsa Harit Gram Yojna Plant Distribution Models

Jharkhand sarkar ne zameen ke area ke according plant distribution ke do important models fix kiye hai:

1. Standard 1-Acre Model (192 Total Plants)

Agar kisan ke paas 1 acre (100 decimal) contiguous zameen hai, toh usme total 192 plants lagaye jaate hain:

2. Standard Unit Model (100 Mixed Plants Model)

Chhote patches (50 decimal ya kam zameen) ke liye 100 plants ka unit model follow kiya jata hai:

  • Grafted Mango (Kalmi Aam): 24 Paudhe
  • Layered Guava (Amrood): 24 Paudhe
  • Lemon (Nimbu): 25 Paudhe
  • Sahjan (Drumstick): 5 Paudhe
  • Timber / Forest Trees: 22 Paudhe (Boundary par)

Types of Saplings Provided under BHGY

Districts me National Horticulture Board (NHB) dwara certified local nurseries se uchh kism ke paudhe khareede jaate hain:

Fruit Varieties: Amrapali & Mallika Mango (Aam), L-49 / Allahabad Safeda Guava (Amrood), Kagzi Lemon (Nimbu), aur Mungia Sahjan.

Timber/Forest Varieties: Arjun, Asan, Mahogani, Sagwan, aur Kusum jo boundary protection aur additional wood income dete hai.

Step-by-Step Plant Distribution & Execution Process

[ Gram Sabha Approval ] ──► [ Pit Digging & CPT Trench ] ──► [ Nursery Sapling Distribution ]
                                                                             │
[ Wage Credit via DBT ] ◄── [ 3-5 Year Maintenance ] ◄── [ Plantation & H-Staking ]
  1. Aavedan & Gram Sabha Selection: Gram Panchayat level par Sakhi Mandal ya Mukhiya ke dwara aavedan liya jata hai. Gram Sabha dwara eligible kisano ke naam approve kiye jaate hai.
  2. Site Preparation: Pit digging aur Cattle Proof Trench (CPT) ka kaam monsoon se pehle May mahine tak complete kiya jata hai.
  3. Saplings Transport & Distribution: Block / DRDA level par empanelled nurseries se saplings direct kisano ke khet tak pahucheye jaate hain.
  4. Plantation & H-Staking: Paudho ki ropai ke baad unhe hawa se bachane ke liye bamboo H-staking aur jalkund (water collection pit) banaye jaate hain.
  5. Wage Payment: Gaddhe khodne, paudha lagane aur pani dene ka majdoori ka paisa direct kisan ke MGNREGA Job Card account me credit hota hai.

Eligibility Criteria & Required Documents

Eligibility

  • Applicant Jharkhand ka permanent niwasi hona chahiye.
  • Priority Groups: SC/ST, Aadim Janjati (PVTG), BPL families, Women-headed households, PMAY beneficiaries, aur Single-parent families.
  • Applicant ke paas valid MGNREGA Job Card hona compulsory hai.
  • Minimum 25 decimal se 1 acre tak uplands honi chahiye.

Required Documents

  • MGNREGA Job Card Copy
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Passbook (Aadhaar linked for wage DBT)
  • Zameen ka Lagan Rasid / Khatian (or Van Patta)
  • Passport-size Photograph

Common Problems & Solutions

1. Problem: “Paudhe Sukh Jaane Par Replacement Nahi Milna”

  • Solution: Scheme ke under 10% to 15% mortality replacement reserve rehta hai. Pehle season me paudha sukhne par aap apne MGNREGA Rojgar Sevak ya Panchayat Mate ko inform karke free replacement sapling claim kar sakte hain.

2. Problem: “MGNREGA Wage Payment Pending”

  • Solution: Apne Job Card ko Aadhaar Seeding aur ABPS (Aadhaar Based Payment System) ke sath bank branch me jaakar update karayein.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Birsa Harit Gram Yojna me 1 acre me kitne paudhe diye jaate hain?

1 acre model ke tehat total 192 paudhe diye jaate hain, jisme 112 faldaar paudhe (Aam/Amrood) aur 80 इमारती (Timber) paudhe shamil hain.

2. Kya paudhe lene ke liye koi charges dene padte hain?

Nahi, Birsa Harit Gram Yojana ke tehat paudhe, khad, fencing aur majdoori 100% free di jaati hai.

3. Fal ugne ke baad unpar kiska adhikar hoga?

Falo aur bagwani se hone wali poori aamdani par sirf aur sirf kisan (beneficiary) ka 100% adhikar hota hai.
