Bihar Board 12th Scrutiny Result 2026 Direct Link: Inter Rechecking Result Ghar Baithe Mobile Se Kaise Check Karein?

Published: 2026-05-21

Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) ke through Class 12th ka annual result jari karne ke baad, jo bache apne marks se satisfied nahi the, unhone apni copies ke Rechecking ka form bhara tha. Lakho students ne ₹120 per subject ki fee dekar aavedan kiya tha aur tabhi se sabhi ko ek hi cheez ka intezar hai, unke badhe hue new marksheet ka.

Agar aap bhi un students mein se hain jinki 1 ya 2 numbers ki wajah se first division chhoot gayi thi ya kisi subject mein cross lag gaya tha, toh aapke liye badi khabar hai. BSEB ne scrutiny ka processing kaam lagbhag poora kar liya hai aur ab kisi bhi waqt result live kiya ja sakta hai.

BSEB Inter Scrutiny Result 2026: Important Timeline

Bihar Board har saal scrutiny ka form bharwane ke lagbhag 25 se 35 dino ke andar result ka status portal par update karna shuru kar deta hai.

Niche diye gaye timeline par ek nazar dalein:

  • Online Scrutiny Form Dates: April 2026 ke pehle hafte tak scrutiny ke aavedan liye gaye the.
  • Copy Rechecking Process: April ke aakhri hafte se lekar May ke pehle hafte tak sabhi checking centers par re-totaling ka kaam chala.
  • Scrutiny Result Release Date: May 2026 ke aakhri hafte (Expected 25th to 30th May) mein result ka portal open kar diya jayega.
  • Result Mode: Yeh result poori tarah online aayega, jahan aapko apna purana Application ID aur Registration Number use karna hoga.

Scrutiny Ke Baad Marks Kaise Badhte Hain?

Kuch bacho ko lagta hai ki scrutiny mein unki copy dubara shuru se check hoti hai, par aisa nahi hai. Bihar Board ke niyam ke according rechecking mein sirf in 3 cheezon par dhyan diya jata hai:

  1. Agar andar ke kisi page par koi question check hone se chhoot gaya ho, toh use check karke marks diye jaate hain.
  2. Answer sheet ke front page par jo total marks jode gaye hain, unki recalculation (re-totaling) ki jati hai ki jodne mein koi galti toh nahi hui.
  3. Agar kisi question par andar 4 marks mile hain par bahar main page par galti se 2 marks chadh gaya ho, toh use theek kiya jata hai.

Step-by-Step Process: 12th Scrutiny Result Kaise Check Karein?

Bihar Board Inter scrutiny ka status check karne ke liye niche diye gaye steps ko dhyan se follow karein:

1.Official Website Open Karein:

Sabse pehle Bihar Board ki official scrutiny portal website biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in ya scrutiny.biharboardonline.com par jayein.

2.Scrutiny Result Link Par Click Karein:

Homepage par aapko ek active link dikhega jisme likha hoga "Inter (Class 12th) Scrutiny Result 2026". Us link par click karein.

3.Login Credentials Enter Karein:

Ab aapke samne ek student login window khulegi. Yahan aapko form bharte waqt mila hua apna Application ID (Scrutiny Application Number) aur apna Registration Number dhyan se enter karna hoga.

4.Login Aur Status Check:

Details daal kar jaise hi aap 'Login' ya 'Submit' button par click karenge, aapka scrutiny dashboard open ho jayega. Wahan aapke un sabhi subjects ki list dikhegi jisme aapne apply kiya tha.

5.Printout Download Karein:

Har subject ke samne aapko status dikhega, jaise - Marks Changed (agar number badhe hain) ya No Change (agar marks pehle jitne hi hain). Is page ka ek screenshot ya PDF download karke apne paas safe rakh lein.

Scrutiny Result Mein Kis Tarah Ke Status Dikhte Hain?

Jab aap apna portal open karenge, toh aapko niche diye gaye remarks mein se koi ek status dikhega:

  • No Change: Iska matlab aapke jodne mein ya checking mein koi galti nahi mili, aapke purane marks hi rahenge.
  • Marks Changed: Iska matlab aapke marks badh chuke hain. Naya number bhi wahan likha hua dikhega.
  • Pending: Agar aisa dikhaye, toh iska matlab abhi aapki copy ka verification process chal raha hai, aapko 24 se 48 ghante baad fir se check karna chahiye.

FAQs: Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

1. Bihar Board 12th Scrutiny Result 2026 direct link kya hai?

Iska official direct link biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in aur scrutiny.biharboardonline.com hai. BSEB isi portal par data live karta hai.

2. Kya scrutiny mein kisi student ke marks ghatt (kam) bhi sakte hain?

Bihar Board ke niyam ke mutabik rechecking mein marks badhte hain ya fir 'No Change' rehte hain. Marks ghattne (decrease hone) ke chances na ke barabar hote hain, isliye darrne ki koi baat nahi hai.

3. Badha hua naya marksheet kab aur kahan se milega?

Scrutiny result aane ke lagbhag 15 se 20 din baad Bihar Board sabhi schools aur colleges mein naya updated marksheet bhejta hai. Aapko apne purane college ja kar purana marksheet jama karna hoga aur naya marksheet receive karna hoga.

4. Agar scrutiny ke baad bhi marks nahi badhe aur main fail hoon toh kya karu?

Agar scrutiny mein 'No Change' aata hai aur aap abhi bhi kisi subject mein fail hain, toh aapko apna saal bachane ke liye BSEB Compartmental Exam 2026 ka form bharna chahiye, jiska exam jaldi hi conduct kiya jata hai.

5. Kya rechecking ka result sabhi bacho ka ek hi din aata hai?

Nahi, Bihar Board rolling basis par result jari karta hai. Ho sakta hai aapke dost ka status aaj show kare aur aapka 'Pending' dikhaye. Isliye har 1-2 din par portal login karke check karte rahein.
