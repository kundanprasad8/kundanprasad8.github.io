NSP Scholarship OTR Registration Process Step by Step: Full Guide 2026

Published: 2026-05-18

Kya aap bhi NSP par scholarship apply karne ki koshish kar rahe hain, lekin har baar wahi purana “OTR Registration” ka chakkar aapko pareshan kar raha hai?

Pehle kya hota tha ki hum har saal naya form bharte the, lekin ab sarkar ne system thoda badal diya hai. Ab aapko baar-baar mehnat nahi karni padegi, bas ek baar NSP scholarship OTR registration process complete karna hai aur aapka kaam ho gaya.

Main khud jab pichle mahine ek Ranchi ke student ka form bhar raha tha, toh mujhe samajh aaya ki ye dikhne mein jitna complex lagta hai, utna hai nahi. Bas kuch baaton ka dhyaan rakhna padta hai varna “Face Authentication” fail ho jata hai aur student pareshan hote rehte hain.

NSP OTR Kya Hai aur Ye Kyun Jaruri Hai?

Simple words mein kahein toh OTR ka matlab hai One-Time Registration. Ye ek 14-digit ka unique number hota hai jo aapki pehchaan ban jata hai pure scholarship portal par.

Pehle kya hota tha ki har baar login karne ke liye alag-alag IDs banani padti thi, jisse data leak ya duplication ka khatra rehta tha. Ab ek baar aapne ye ID bana li, toh phir chahe aap Pre-Matric scholarship bharein ya Post-Matric, aapko baar-baar apni personal details enter karne ki jarurat nahi padegi.

Ye system isliye laya gaya hai taaki fake applications ko roka ja sake aur sahi bacho tak direct benefit transfer (DBT) ke jariye paisa pahunche.

Documents List

Registration shuru karne se pehle ye list check kar lein taaki beech mein process na ruke. Agar ek bhi cheez missing hui toh aapka session expire ho sakta hai.

  • Aadhaar Card: Sabse important document. Isme aapka naam aur DOB wahi honi chahiye jo school certificates mein hai.
  • Aadhaar Linked Mobile Number: Bina OTP ke process aage nahi badhega. Agar mobile number purana hai ya band ho gaya hai, toh pehle use update karayein.
  • Active Email ID: Future updates ke liye ye bahut kaam aati hai.
  • Android Phone: Kyunki face authentication ke liye aapko mobile app ki jarurat padegi. Desktop se ye kaam thoda mushkil ho jata hai.

NSP Scholarship OTR Registration Process Step by Step

Ab aate hain asli kaam par. Step-by-step process bahut simple hai agar aap ise dhang se follow karein. Ise humne 4 bade steps mein divide kiya hai.

Step 1: NSP OTR App aur Face RD App Install Karein

Sabse pehle apne Play Store par jaiye. Wahan se aapko do apps download karni hain:

  1. NSP OTR App: Ye official app hai registration ke liye.
  2. AadhaarFaceRD App: Ye UIDAI ki official app hai face scan karne ke liye.

Step 2: Reference Number Generate Karein

App open karne ke baad ‘Register’ button par click karein. Yahan aapko apna Aadhaar-linked mobile number dalna hoga. Ek OTP aayega, use verify kijiye. Iske baad aapko apni Aadhaar details deni hogi.

Details match hone ke baad system aapko ek Reference Number generate karke dega. Is number ko kahin note kar lein ya screenshot le lein kyunki ye login ke waqt kaam aayega.

Step 3: Face Authentication Kaise Karein?

Ab asli magic shuru hota hai. Aapko app mein ‘e-KYC with FaceAuth’ ka option dikhega. Wahan apna Reference Number daliye. Ab aapka camera open hoga.

Dhyaan dene wali baatein:

  • Lighting: Kamre mein achhi light honi chahiye. Face par shadow nahi aani chahiye.
  • Blink Test: Camera ke samne aapko apni aankhein thodi blink (jhapkana) karni padengi. Isse system ko pata chalta hai ki aap “live” person hain, koi photo nahi.
  • Background: Koshish karein ki piche ki deewar plain ho, taaki focus sirf aapke face par rahe.

Step 4: OTR ID aur Password Receive Karein

Jaise hi face scan successful hoga, aapki screen par 14-digit OTR ID dikh jayegi. Saath hi aapke mobile number par ek SMS aayega jisme aapka default password hoga. Ab aap portal par login karne ke liye taiyar hain.

OTR ID Milne Ke Baad Portal Par Login Kaise Karein?

Registration hone ka matlab ye nahi hai ki scholarship apply ho gayi. Ye toh bas aapki digital profile bani hai. Scholarship pane ke liye aapko ye steps follow karne honge:

  1. Sabse pehle official NSP portal (scholarships.gov.in) par jayein.
  2. ‘Apply for Fresh’ ya ‘Login’ section mein jayein.
  3. Apni 14-digit OTR ID aur mobile par aaye password se login karein.
  4. First time login par aapko password change karne ka option milega, ek strong password bana lein.
  5. Ab aapka dashboard khul jayega. Wahan apni scheme select karein aur form bharna shuru karein.

Common Problems aur Unke Solutions

Kai baar students ko NSP scholarship OTR registration process ke dauran kuch technical issues aate hain. Humne kuch common problems yahan share kiye hain:

  • OTP Not Received: Agar OTP nahi aa raha, toh check karein ki kya wahi number Aadhaar se linked hai. Kabhi-kabhi network issue ki wajah se 2-3 minute lag sakte hain, jaldbazi mein ‘Resend’ baar-baar na dabayein.
  • Face Auth Failed: Agar baar-baar fail ho raha hai, toh apna phone restart karein aur FaceRD app ko permissions check karein. Koshish karein ki bina chashme (glasses) ke photo click karein.
  • Data Mismatch: Agar Aadhaar mein naam ‘Rahul Kumar’ hai aur school record mein ‘Rahul Singh’, toh OTR reject ho sakta hai. Dono jagah details same karana bahut jaruri hai.

Scholarship Form Bharte Waqt Kya Dhyaan Rakhein?

Jab aap login kar lete hain, toh baaki ki details dhyan se bharein:

  • Income Certificate: Income wahi bharein jo certificate mein hai.
  • Bank Account: Ab toh dbt (direct benefit transfer) ka zamana hai, isliye wahi account de jo Aadhaar se seed (linked) ho.
  • Scheme Selection: Apni caste aur category ke hisab se sahi scheme chunein. Galat scheme chunne par application reject ho sakti hai.

FAQs: Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

1. Kya purani ID se kaam chal jayega ya OTR jaruri hai? Nahi, ab purani Application ID kaam nahi karegi. Sabhi students ko (chahe woh fresh ho ya renewal) naya OTR registration karna hi padega.

2. Face Authentication ke liye kaun sa app best hai? Aapko Play Store se AadhaarFaceRD app hi use karni chahiye, kyunki ye UIDAI ka official software hai jo NSP app ke saath connect hokar kaam karta hai.

3. OTR ID generate hone mein kitna time lagta hai? Agar aapka face authentication aur OTP verification sahi se ho jata hai, toh OTR ID turant (instantly) screen par dikh jati hai.

4. Kya main bina Aadhaar ke registration kar sakta hoon? Filhaal NSP ne Aadhaar ko mandatory kar diya hai. Agar aapke paas Aadhaar nahi hai, toh aapko enrollment slip se try karna hoga, lekin scholarship aane ke liye Aadhaar link hona jaruri hai.

5. Agar mera OTR password kho jaye toh kya karein? Aap NSP portal par ‘Forgot Password’ par click karke apna OTR ID aur registered mobile number daal kar naya password mangwa sakte hain.
