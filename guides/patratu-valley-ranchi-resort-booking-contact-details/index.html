Patratu Valley Ranchi Resort Booking Contact Details: Complete Visitor Guide

Published: 2026-08-04

Ranchi se around 35-40 km ki doori par located Patratu Valley aur Patratu Dam Jharkhand ke sabse popular tourist destinations mei se ek hai. Ghumaavdaar spiral road, charo taraf gheri green hills, aur Patratu lake ka huge reservoir ise ek magical weekend getaway banata hai.

Distance & Route Guide

  • From Ranchi City Center: Approx. 35 – 40 km via Pithoria – Patratu Ghat Road.
  • From Birsa Munda Airport Ranchi: Approx. 45 km (approx. 1.5 hours drive).
  • From Ranchi Railway Station: Approx. 42 km.
  • Route Highlight: Famous for its 30+ hairpin S-curves down the valley. Drive carefully, especially during heavy fog or monsoon rain.

Top Things to Experience at Patratu

  • Patratu Valley Viewpoint: Panoramic hilltop vantage points to photograph the serpentine ghat roads.
  • Patratu Dam & Lake Promenade: Well-paved lakeside walking path with sitting areas, food courts, and children’s park.
  • Island Park & Boating: Island park inside the reservoir accessible via motorboat.
  • Water Sports: Speedboats, jet skis, and pedal boats managed under tourism safety regulations.
Pro Visitor Tip: Plan your drive through Patratu Ghat during sunrise or late afternoon (3:30 PM – 5:30 PM) to catch sunset views over the reservoir. Weekend rushes can cause parking delays near the main dam entrance.

How to Reach Patratu Valley & Lake Resort from Ranchi

  • By Car / Two-Wheeler (Most Recommended): Ranchi city Center (Kanke Road) se Pithoria Ghat hote hue Patratu Valley tak ka safar lagbhag 35-40 km hai. Pithoria Ghat ke winding roads par drive karna ek thrilling experience hai.
  • By Bus: Ranchi ITI Bus Stand ya Khadgara Bus Stand se Patratu/Ramgarh ke liye regular local buses mil jati hai.
  • By Train: Patratu Railway Station (PTRU) nearest railway station hai jo lagbhag 8-10 km ki doori par hai.

Things to Keep in Mind Before Visiting

  • ID Proof Mandatory: Check-in ke dauran sabhi adult guests ke paas valid Government ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving License) hona compulsory hai.
  • Avoid Driving Late Night: Pithoria Ghat section par raat 9:00 PM ke baad drive karna avoid karein kyunki fog aur winding curves ke karan visibility kam ho sakti hai.
  • Boating Ticket Separate Hai: Resort booking mein boating charges include nahi hote hain. Boating ke liye aapko Lake Complex counters par alag se ticket leni hoti hai.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Patratu Lake Resort (Sarovar Vihar) ka official booking contact number kya hai?

Patratu Lake Resort ke manager ka direct official contact number +91 9905900149 hai aur Central JTDC desk number 9102403884 hai.

2. Kya Patratu Lake Resort par offline/spot booking ho sakti hai?

Haan, yadi rooms vacant hain toh aap spot booking kar sakte hain. Lekin weekends par peak rush ke karan online advance booking karwana hi recommended rehta hai.

3. Patratu Valley ke resorts mein per night room rate kitna hai?

Average room tariff ₹2,500 se ₹7,500 per night ke beech rehta hai, jo room type (Standard AC vs Lake View Villa) par depend karta hai.

4. Kya unmarried couples ke liye Patratu Lake Resort safe hai?

JTDC operated Sarovar Vihar mein valid adult Government ID proof dikhane par policy ke tehat standard norms follow kiye jaate hain. Always local guidelines confirm kar lein.

5. Patratu Valley visit karne ka best season konsa hai?

October se March ke beech ka mausam Patratu Valley visit karne ke liye sabse best hota hai. Monsoons (July-September) mein bhi valley ka green cover dekhne laayak hota hai.
