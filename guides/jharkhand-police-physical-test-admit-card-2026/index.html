Jharkhand Police Physical Test Admit Card 2026: PET/PST Date Aur Direct Download Link Ki Poori Jankari

Published: 2026-05-24

Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) ke through conduct ki jaane wali Jharkhand Police Constable recruitment exam ki taiyari kar rahe lakhon candidates ke liye ek behad zaroori update hai. Jharkhand Police mein Constable banne ka sapna dekhne wale bacho ka sabse pehla aur bada mukabla Physical Efficiency Test (PET) aur Physical Standard Test (PST) ke roop mein hone wala hai.

Desh aur rajya ke niyam ke according, Jharkhand Police Constable selection process mein pehle written exam na hokar, sabse pehle aapka Physical Ground Test liya jata hai. Is physical test mein qualify karne wale bacho ko hi agle stage (Medical aur Written Test) ke liye bulaya jayega. Agar aap bhi is ground test ki din-raat taiyari kar rahe hain aur jharkhand police physical test admit card 2026 download link ke baare mein janana chahte hain, toh is article ko dhyan se poora padhein.

Jharkhand Police Physical Test 2026 Overview

Ground par daud shuru hone se pehle is recruitment cycle ki saari basic details ko ek baar short me samajh lete hain:

Physical Endurance Test (PET): Kya Honge Ground Ke Rules?

Jharkhand Police Constable aur Kakshpal (Jail Warder) posts ke liye physical endurance ke standard kaafi kade hote hain. Ground test clear karne ke liye aapko niche diye gaye parameters ko qualify karna hoga:

1. Running Test

  • Male Candidates: 10 Kilometers ki daud poori karni hogi 60 minutes (1 ghante) ke andar.
  • Female Candidates: 5 Kilometers ki daud poori karni hogi 30 minutes ke andar.

2. Height aur Chest (Physical Standards)

  • General/OBC (Male): Minimum Height 160 cm, Chest kam se kam 81 cm hona chahiye.
  • SC/ST (Male): Minimum Height 155 cm, Chest kam se kam 79 cm hona chahiye.
  • All Categories (Female): Minimum Height 148 cm honi chahiye

Step-by-Step Guide: Physical Test Admit Card Kaise Download Karein?

JSSC jab bhi physical test ki dates announce karega, uske thik 7 se 10 din pehle admit card download karne ka official link server par live ho jayega. Admit card download karne ka sahi process niche diya gaya hai:

1.JSSC ki Main Website Par Jayein:

Sabse pehle apne smartphone ya laptop par Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission ki official website jssc.jharkhand.gov.in open karein.

2.Admit Card Link Ko Locate Karein:

Homepage par menu section mein dikh rahe “Admit Card” wale portal par click karein. Wahan aapko “Link to Download Admit Card for Jharkhand Police Constable PET-2026” ka link dikhega, use open karein.

3.Registration details Enter Karein:

Ab khule hue login page par apna Registration Number aur Date of Birth (DD-MM-YYYY) fill karein. Neeche diye gaye visual captcha code ko box mein barabar type karein.

4.Admit Card Printout Nikalein:

“Login” button par click karte hi aapka physical test call letter screen par aa jayega. Is PDF ko download karein aur iska ek clean Color Printout nikal kar apne paas rakh lein.

Direct Links for Candidates

  • Official Web Portal: jssc.jharkhand.gov.in
  • Admit Card Direct Server Link: jssc.nic.in/admitcard (Link active hote hi login dashboard open ho jayega)

FAQs: Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

1. Kya Jharkhand Police mein pehle written exam hoga ya physical test?

Jharkhand Police selection criteria ke naye niyam ke anusar, sabse pehle aapka Physical Test (PET/PST) liya jayega. Jo bache physical daud aur measurement paas karenge, sirf unhi ko medical aur phir written exam mein baithne ka mauka milega.

2. Physical admit card download karne ke liye kya detail chahiye?

Aapko apna form bharte waqt mila hua Online Registration Number aur apni Date of Birth (DOB) ki zaroorat padegi.

3. Kya biometric mismatch hone par ground se bahar kar diya jata hai?

Haan, agar aapka fingerprint ya biometric data Aadhar se match nahi karta hai, toh verification roki ja sakti hai. Isliye dhyan rakhein ki aapka Aadhar card active ho aur aapke details correct hon.

4. Daud ke samay kya hosh me rakhna zaroori hai?

Daud ke dauran aapko ek digital chip lagayi ja sakti hai jo aapke rounds ko track karti hai. Har round ko track karne ke liye commission ke nirdeshon ko ground par dhyan se sunein.
