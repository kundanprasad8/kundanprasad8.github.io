Jharkhand Bijli Bill Waiver Scheme 2026: 200 Unit Free Bijli Aur Purana Bill Maaf

Published: 2026-05-12

Jharkhand mein garmi aate hi coolers aur AC full speed mein chalne lagte hain, jisse mahine ke aakhiri mein aane wala bijli ka bill kisi ko bhi tension mein daal sakta hai. Lekin pichle kuch waqt se JBVNL (Jharkhand Bijli Vitran Nigam Limited) ke consumers ke liye ek badi rahat wali khabar chal rahi hai. Hemant Soren sarkar ne “Mukhyamantri Urja Khushhali Yojana” ke tahat Jharkhand Bijli Bill Waiver Scheme 2026 lagu kar di hai.

Is scheme ka sidha sa fayda yeh hai ki ab aapke ghar ka bill zero aa sakta hai. Jo log search kar rahe hain ki aakhir ye 200 unit free bijli ka niyam kya hai aur purane pending bill mafi ka kya process hai, unke liye maine ye poori detail tayar ki hai. Aao dekhte hain ki iska fayda aap kaise le sakte ho.

Is table mein poori scheme ka ek chota sa overview dekh lo:

Kya Hai 200 Unit Free Electricity Scheme?

Pehle Jharkhand mein sirf 100 unit tak ki bijli free milti thi. Lekin ab sarkar ne is limit ko double karke 200 unit kar diya hai. Matlab agar aapke ghar ka meter ek mahine mein 200 unit se kam bijli khinchta hai, toh aapko ek rupaya bhi nahi dena hoga. JBVNL isme energy charge, fixed charge aur electricity duty sab kuch poori tarah se hata deta hai. Aaj ke time mein lagbhag 40 lakh parivaron ko iska direct fayda mil raha hai.

Kise Milega Is Bijli Bill Mafi Ka Fayda?

Boht se log puchte hain ki kya dukaano par bhi ye scheme chalegi. Ek baat yaad rakhna, agar aap Ranchi mein Shri Paras Marble jaisi kisi badi company ya showroom mein kaam karte hain aur wahan ka meter bill dekhte hain, toh wahan commercial rate hi lagega. Commercial jagahon par koi chhut nahi milti.

Is scheme ka poora fayda lene ke liye ye baatein zaroori hain:

  • Aapka bijli connection ‘Domestic’ yani sirf gharelu hona chahiye.
  • Aap Jharkhand ke permanent resident hone chahiye.
  • Ek mahine ki total meter reading 200 unit ke andar hi honi chahiye.

Purana Bakaaya (Pending) Bill Ka Kya Hoga?

Sabse badi pareshani un logon ki thi jinka pichle kai saalon ka bill hazaron rupaye baki tha. Sarkar ne iska bhi tod nikal diya hai. Is yojana ke under purane saare bakaaye (arrears) aur uspar lagne wala late fine completely maaf kar diya gaya hai.

Ranchi, Hazaribagh, Jamshedpur aur baki zilon mein JBVNL ki taraf se special camps lagaye gaye the jahan logon ko “Bijli Bill Mafi Praman Patra” (Waiver Certificates) baante gaye. Agar aapka bhi purana bill baki tha aur ab aap 200 unit ke andar khapat kar rahe hain, toh aapka picchla saara hisaab zero kar diya gaya hai.

Zero Bill Kaise Aata Hai? Kya Koi Form Bharna Hoga?

Ye is scheme ki sabse badhiya cheez hai. Aapko block office ya JBVNL ke chakkar lagakar koi lamba-chauda form nahi bharna hai. Ye poora process ekdum automatic hai.

Jab Urja Mitra (meter reading lene aane wale bhaiya) aapke ghar aakar apni machine mein reading dalte hain, toh system turant check karta hai. Agar wahan reading 190 ya 195 unit aati hai, toh aapki receipt par total amount 0.00 Rs print ho kar nikal aayega. Aapke phone par jo SMS aayega, usme bhi bill amount zero hi likha hoga.

Agar 200 Unit Se Zyada Bijli Uth Gayi Toh?

Socho agar aapke ghar mein AC ya geyser laga hai aur kisi mahine aapka bill 215 unit aa gaya. Aise mein aapko subsidy ka fayda nahi milega aur aapko poore bill ka payment karna padega. Isiliye koshish karna ki LED lights aur energy-saving appliances ka use karke apna consumption hamesha 200 ke andar hi rakho.

Smart Meter Walo Ke Liye Zaroori Baat: Aajkal Ranchi aur dusre bade shaharon mein purane meter hata kar naye smart prepaid meter lagaye ja rahe hain. Kafi log tension mein the ki prepaid meter mein toh roz paisa katta hai, phir wahan free bijli kaise milegi. Darrne ki baat nahi hai, wahan bhi system same hai. JBVNL ka software aapke smart meter mein 200 unit tak ka balance automatic adjust kar leta hai. Jab tak aap 200 unit cross nahi karte, aapka main balance deduct nahi hota.

Faltu chal rahe pankhe aur light band rakhne ki aadat daal lo taaki mahine ke end mein lamba bill na pakdana pade aur har mahine 200 unit wali free scheme ka aaram se fayda utha sako.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Jharkhand mein 2026 mein kitne unit tak bijli free hai?

Ans: Jharkhand mein gharelu connection walon ke liye har mahine 200 unit tak bijli ekdum free hai. Isme energy aur fixed charges dono maaf hain.

Q2. Kya mujhe bijli bill mafi ke liye koi online form bharna hoga?

Ans: Bilkul nahi. JBVNL ka system apne aap aapka bill zero kar deta hai. Agar aap 200 unit se kam bijli use karte hain, toh automatic zero rupaye ki receipt nikal aayegi.

Q3. Mera purana bijli bill boht baki hai, uska kya hoga?

Ans: Sarkar ne Mukhyamantri Urja Khushhali Yojana ke under purane saare bakaaye aur fine maaf kar diye hain. Iska labh un sabhi logon ko mil raha hai jo 200 unit ke andar rahte hain.

Q4. Kya dukaan ya factory wale meter par bhi free bijli milegi?

Ans: Nahi, yeh scheme sirf gharelu (domestic) upbhoktaon ke liye banayi gayi hai. Dukaan, factory ya kisi commercial meter par aapko poora bill dena hoga.

Q5. Zero bill aane par kya meter reading wale bhaiya ghar aayenge?

Ans: Haan, Urja Mitra har mahine aapke ghar aakar meter reading zarur lenge, taaki unhe aur system ko pata chal sake ki aapka unit 200 ke andar hai ya nahi.
