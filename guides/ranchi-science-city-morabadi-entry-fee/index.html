Ranchi Science City Morabadi Entry Fee: Complete Visitor Guide & Timings

Published: 2026-08-01

Ranchi ke popular Morabadi area mei Tagore Hill ke paas located Regional Science Centre (jise local log Ranchi Science City ya Morabadi Science Centre ke naam se jaante hai) bachchon, students aur science enthusiasts ke liye ek aacha aur educational spot hai. Around 13 acre ke green area mei phaila yeh centre National Council of Science Museums ke through operate kiya jata hai.

Agar aap apne family, school group ya bacchon ke saath Ranchi Science City ghoomne ka plan bana rahe hai, toh yeh article aapko Ranchi Science City Morabadi Entry Fee, closed days, show timings (3D show & Planetarium), parking charges, aur ghoomne ki poori information provide karega.

Opening Hours & Weekly Closed Day

  • Operating Hours: 10:30 AM to 5:30 PM (Ticket counter closes by 5:00 PM).
  • Weekly Off: Monday Closed (Remains closed on major gazetted holidays).
  • Best Time to Visit: 11:30 AM to 3:30 PM to cover all shows comfortably.

Show Schedules (Approx.)

  • 3D Theater Shows: Multiple regular shows between 11:30 AM and 4:30 PM.
  • Taramandal (Planetarium) Shows: Batch-wise shows conducted based on group strength.
  • Science Demonstration: Special science experiments conducted for school groups on prior request.

Major Attractions inside Science City

  • Fun Science Gallery: Interactive exhibits on optics, sound, mechanics, and illusions.
  • 3D Science Theater: Immersive 3D movie experience focusing on science and space.
  • Taramandal (Inflatable Planetarium): Live night-sky star gazing simulation show.
  • Outdoor Science Park: Large green open park with interactive physical exhibits, play equipment, and life-size dinosaur models.
  • Jharkhand Energy Education Gallery: Educational models on renewable energy and coal mining.
Visitor Tip: Located right near Tagor Hill / Morabadi Ground in Ranchi. School groups can request concessions by submitting an official letter from the school principal at the booking counter.

Overview of Ranchi Science City

Science City Morabadi mei interactive science exhibits, Fun Science Gallery, 3D Theater, Planetarium, aur outdoor Science Park present hai, jahan khel-khel mei science sikhe ja sakte hai.

Closed Days & Operating Timings

Ranchi Science City visit karne se pehle iska weekly off day aur timing janna zaroori hai:

Closed Days

  • Weekly Off: Every Monday Ranchi Science City poori tarah BAND rehta hai.
  • National Holidays: Deepawali, Holi, Republic Day (26 Jan), aur Independence Day (15 Aug) jaise important festival par bhi centre closed reh sakta hai.

Daily Timings

  • Opening Time: Subah 10:00 AM
  • Closing Time: Shaam 5:00 PM
  • Ticket Counter Timing: 10:00 AM se 4:30 PM tak

Key Attractions inside Morabadi Science City

  1. Fun Science Gallery: 50 se zyada interactive exhibits jahan optics, mechanics, sound, aur electricity ke principles ko khud try karke dekha ja sakta hai.
  2. 3D Theater: Special 3D glasses ke sath nature, dinosaurs, aur space par adharit exciting short films.
  3. Taramandal (Planetarium Show): Ek inflatable dome theater jahan raat ke aakash, taaron (stars), aur planets ke baare mein samjhaaya jata hai.
  4. Outdoor Science Park: Open-air lush green garden jahan vishal science models, whisper dishes, aur lever mechanisms lage hue hain jahan bacche khelte huye seekhte hai.
  5. Mock Coal Mine & Geology Corner: Jharkhand ke mineral wealth aur coal mining process ko darshane wala dedicated section.

How to Reach Ranchi Science City Morabadi

Morabadi Science City Ranchi city ke heart mein located hai aur aaram se reachable hai:

  • From Main Road / Overbridge: Distance approx 7-8 km. Shared auto ya cab (Ola/Uber/InDrive) se Morabadi ya Tagore Hill tak direct auto mil jaate hai.
  • From Ranchi Railway Station: Distance approx 9 km. Station se Morabadi ke liye autos aur e-rickshaws easily available hai.
  • From Birsa Munda Airport: Distance approx 14 km. Ring road ya Kanke road route se 30 minute mein pahuncha ja sakta hai.

Guidelines for Visitors

  • Camera & Mobiles Allowed: Gallery ke andar photography allowed hai, lekin 3D show ke time pe flash use na karein.
  • Food & Drinks: Science galleries ke andar khane-peene ki chizein le jana mana hai. Campus ke andar chota canteen/snack counter present hai.
  • School Bus Parking: Campus ke bahar dedicated parking space available hai jahan bus aur cars park ki ja sakti hai.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Ranchi Science City Morabadi ka entry fee kitna hai?

Ranchi Science City ke general entry ticket ka price Adults ke liye ₹25 se ₹30 per person hai.

2. Kya Ranchi Science City Monday ko khula rehta hai?

Nahi, Ranchi Science City har Monday (Somwar) ko poori tarah band rehta hai. Monday ko chhodkar baki sabhi din yeh khula rehta hai.

3. Ranchi Science City mein 3D Show ka ticket price kya hai?

3D Science Film Show ka ticket price lagbhag ₹20 se ₹25 per person hai.

4. Science City Morabadi ki timing kya hai?

Ranchi Science City subah 10:00 AM se shaam 5:00 PM tak khula rehta hai. Ticket counter 4:30 PM par band ho jata hai.

5. Kya Ranchi Science City ke paas Tagore Hill hai?

Haan, Science City Tagor Hill (Chiraundi, Morabadi) ke bilkul paas sthit hai. Aap ek hi trip mein dono places visit kar sakte hain.
