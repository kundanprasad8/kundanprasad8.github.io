🔔 Shravan Maas & Shivratri Schedule

• Sawan Opening Time: 4:00 AM
• Arghya Jalabhishek: Non-stop queue system for offering water.
• Shravani Mela: Month-long fair managed by District Administration Khunti with drinking water, medical tents, and police booths.
• Maha Shivratri: Temple remains open continuously overnight.

🚉 Nearest Railway Stations & Airport

1. Hatia Railway Station (HTE) — 38 km: Closest major railhead with connectivity across India. Direct buses, taxis, and shared autos to Khunti are easily available outside the station.

2. Ranchi Junction (RNC) — 45 km: Main railway terminal. Take an auto/bus to Birsa Chowk or Khadgara Bus Stand to catch Khunti-Torpa buses.

3. Birsa Munda Airport Ranchi (IXR) — 40 km: Nearest commercial airport. Direct prepaid taxis available to Angrabari via Ring Road & NH-20.

4. Govindpur Road Station (GDB) — 18 km: Local passenger rail stop on the Ranchi-Rourkela route.

🛕 The Open-Air Swayambhu Shivling & Legend

• Why No Roof Above the Shivling? According to local tradition, every time villagers or kings attempted to build a roof over the main Shivling, Lord Shiva appeared in their dreams instructing them to keep the shrine under the open sky. Today, the sacred self-manifested (Swayambhu) Shivling rests naturally beneath a sacred Banyan tree.

• Naming by Shankaracharya: Originally known as Angrabari, the shrine complex was named Amreshwar Dham by Jagadguru Shankaracharya Swami Swarupananda Saraswati.

• Mini Baba Dham Status: Considered the most sacred Shiva pilgrimage site in Chota Nagpur plateau, housing shrines of Lord Shiva, Maa Parvati, Lord Ram-Sita, Hanuman Ji, and Radha-Krishna.

💡 Side Trip Suggestion: Combine your visit with Panchghagh Falls (16 km past Torpa) or Dassam Falls for a day tour.

1. By Road (Car, Bike, or Bus)

  • Ranchi to Khunti Stretch (NH-20): Ranchi City (Birsa Chowk) se nikal kar NH-20 (Ranchi-Chaibasa Highway) pakdein. Hatia cross karne ke baad lagbhag 30 km ki smooth 4-lane Highway drive ke baad aap Khunti town pahunch jayenge.
  • Khunti to Angrabari Turn: Khunti Town Main Market / Chowk se aapko Torpa Road ki taraf turn lena hoga.
  • Final Destination: Khunti-Torpa Road par lagbhag 9 km drive karne ke baad roadside par Angrabari Amreshwar Dham ka main entrance gate aur parking area mil jayega.
  • Bus Transport: Ranchi (Khadgara / ITI Bus Stand / Birsa Chowk) se Khunti aur Simdega / Torpa jane wali har local aur private bus aapko Khunti Chowk ya direct Angrabari turn par drop kar sakti hai.

Nearest Railway Stations & Airport

1. Nearest Railway Stations

  • Hatia Railway Station (HTE): Amreshwar Dham ka sabse nearest major railhead Hatia Station hai, jo temple se lagbhag 30 km door hai. Station ke bahar se direct rental cabs, shared autos, aur buses Khunti ke liye easily mil jaati hain.
  • Ranchi Junction (RNC): Main Ranchi Station se temple ki distance lagbhag 40 km hai.

2. Nearest Airport

  • Birsa Munda Airport, Ranchi (IXR): Temple se lagbhag 32 km – 35 km ki doori par hai. Airport se direct cab hire karke NH-20 ke raste 45 minutes me Amreshwar Dham pahuncha ja sakta hai.

Key Highlights & Temple Timings

  • Open-Air Shivling: Yahan Shivling kisi band garbhagriha me nahi, balki vishal Banyan (Bargad) aur Peepal trees ke niche khule aasmaan ke niche sthapit hai.
  • Temple History & Naming: Sharda Peeth ke Jagadguru Shankaracharya Swami Swaroopanand Saraswati ne is sthal ki spiritual importance ko dekhte hue ise Amreshwar Dham naam diya tha.
  • Other Deities: Main Shivling ke ilawa temple complex me Goddess Durga, Lord Ram-Sita, Lord Hanuman, aur Lord Ganesha ke dedicated temples hain.
  • Daily Timings: Temple daily subah 06:00 AM se shaam 08:00 PM tak khula rehta hai. Sawan Maas aur Shivaratri ke din gate subah 04:00 AM hi khol diye jaate hain.
