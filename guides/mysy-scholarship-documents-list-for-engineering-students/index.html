MYSY Scholarship Documents List for Engineering Students: Form Reject Hone Se Bachayein

Published: 2026-05-19

Kya aap Gujarat mein B.E. ya B.Tech kar rahe hain aur Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana (MYSY) ka form bharna chahte hain? Engineering ki fees itni zyada hoti hai ki aisi sarkari madad kisi bhi student ke liye ek badi help hoti hai. Lekin kya aapko pata hai ki MYSY scholarship mein sabse zyada applications sirf choti si document errors ki wajah se reject ho jati hain?

Engineering students ka data verify karne ke liye system bahut strict rules follow karta hai. Main pichle saal Ahmedabad ke ek engineering college ke group ka form bharwa raha tha. Unmein se do bacho ka form sirf isliye atak gaya kyunki unhone ACPC ka galat endorsement letter upload kar diya tha.

MYSY Scholarship Ke Bare Mein Kuch Zaruri Baatein

MYSY scholarship Gujarat sarkar ki ek behtar scheme hai jo meritorious students ko professional courses ke liye financial help deti hai. Engineering students ko isme har saal unki tuition fee ka 50% (Maximum ₹50,000 per year) tak ka benefit milta hai. Iske alawa, ₹5,000 ka book/instruments grant aur agar aap apne native Taluka se bahar padhte hain toh ₹1,200 per month ka hostel allowance bhi milta hai.

Lekin iska fayda uthane ke liye aapke pass sahi authority ke banaye hue valid papers hone chahiye. Chaliye dekhte hain fresh aur renewal dono cases mein kaun se documents lagenge.

Fresh Engineering Students Ke Liye Mandatory Documents List

Agar aap engineering ke First Year (ya fir Diploma ke baad Direct Second Year D2D) mein hain, toh aapko ‘Fresh Application’ bharni hogi. Iske liye niche diye gaye documents ko original scan karke rakh lein:

  • Aadhaar Card: Aapke Aadhaar ki front aur back side dono ki clear copy honi chahiye.
  • 10th aur 12th Ki Marksheet: Agar aapne 12th Science ke baad B.Tech liya hai toh donon marksheet lagengi. (Dhayan rakhein ki fresh ke liye 12th mein kam se kam 80 Percentile Rank (PR) hona compulsory hai).
  • Diploma Marksheets (D2D ke liye): Agar aap Diploma to Degree student hain, toh aapke pass kam se kam 65% marks ka proof hona chahiye.
  • ACPC Admission Letter: Agar aapka admission ACPC ke zariye hua hai, toh unka official allotment letter lagega.
  • Management Quota / Vacant Seat Documents: Agar aapne MQ ya VQ seat par admission liya hai, toh ACPC ka Endorsement Letter upload karna mandatory hai, iske bina verification nahi hoga.
  • Current College Fee Receipt: Aapne college mein jo tuition fee bhari hai, uski original receipt jisme institute ki seal aur signature ho.
  • Income Certificate: Ye certificate sirf Mamlatdar ya Taluka Development Officer (TDO) ka hi valid mana jata hai. Aapke parivar ki varshik aay ₹6,00,000 se kam honi chahiye.
  • Bonafide Certificate / Institute Certificate: MYSY portal par ek fix format diya hota hai, use print karke apne college ke HOD ya Principal se sign karwa kar taiyar rakhein.
  • Parent’s Self-Declaration Form: Ye bhi website par diye gaye format ke hisab se original upload hoga.
  • Bank Passbook First Page: Ya fir student ke naam ka cancel cheque, jisme IFSC code aur account number bilkul saaf dikh raha ho.
  • Ration Card: Iska pehla aur aakhri page jahan parivar ke sadasyon ke naam hote hain.

Renewal Application Ke Liye Kaun Se Documents Lagenge?

Engineering ke agle saalo mein (2nd, 3rd, ya 4th year) scholarship ko continue rakhne ke liye aapko har saal renewal form bharna padta hai. Renewal ke liye MYSY scholarship documents list for engineering students thodi choti hoti hai lekin isme marks maintain rakhna jaruri hai:

  • Previous Year Marksheets: Aapke odd aur even dono semesters ka result pass hona chahiye (Kam se kam 50% marks ke saath, bina kisi back-log ya ATKT ke).
  • Current Year Tuition Fee Receipt: Jo fee aapne naye academic year ke liye bhari hai.
  • Valid Income Certificate: MYSY mein income certificate issue date se 3 financial years tak valid rehta hai, agar wo expire ho gaya hai toh naya lagayein.
  • Renewal Certificate from Institute: College se sign karwaya hua MYSY ka specific renewal format.
  • Ration Card & Aadhaar Card: Pehle ki tarah iska bhi clear scan lagega.

Hostel Allowance Ke Liye Extra Documents

Agar aap apne native Taluka (apne ghar ke area) se dur kisi dusre district ya taluka mein rehkar engineering kar rahe hain, toh aap ₹12,000 se upar ka hostel grant alag se claim kar sakte hain. Iske liye aapko ye extra papers dene honge:

  • Official Hostel Fee Receipt: Jisme hostel ka registration number, seal, aur revenue stamp laga ho.
  • Registered Rent Agreement: Agar aap bahar kisi room ya PG mein rent par reh rahe hain, toh landlord ke saath kiya hua current year ka valid rent agreement upload karna padega.

3 Badi Galtiyan Jisse Engineering Students Ka Form Reject Hota Hai

  1. Non-Seeded Bank Account: Aapka bank account aapke Aadhaar card se NPCI mapped / seeded hona chahiye. Agar DBT status enable nahi hoga, toh sarkar paisa bhej hi nahi payegi.
  2. Photocopy Scan Uploading: Hamesha original documents ko hi scanner se scan karke upload karein. Xerox copy par dobara scan karne se low-quality ka error aata hai aur query generate ho jati hai.
  3. Income Tax Return (ITR): Agar aapke parents ki income ₹2.5 Lakh se upar hai toh ITR ki copy lagana mat bhooliye, varna scrutiny team application rok deti hai.

FAQs: Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

1. Kya MYSY scholarship ke liye naya income certificate har saal banwana padta hai?

Nahi, Gujarat government ke naye rules ke mutabik ek baar banwaya hua income certificate poore 3 financial years tak valid rehta hai.

2. Agar engineering mein meri ek semester mein ATKT aa gayi toh kya hoga?

MYSY renewal ke liye aapka result clear hona chahiye aur minimum 50% marks hone chahiye. Agar ATKT hai toh jab tak aap use clear karke pass marksheet nahi late, tab tak renewal approve nahi hoga.

3. Kya hostel grant pane ke liye 50 KM dur hona jaruri hai?

Nahi, main rule Taluka change hone ka hai. Agar aapka college aapke native Taluka ke bahar hai, toh aap hostel ya rent allowance ke liye eligible ho jate hain.

4. Kya TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) wale engineering students ko MYSY milti hai?

Nahi, agar aapko pehle se hi TFWS seat mili hai jahan aapki tuition fee zero hai, toh aap MYSY ke tuition fee grant ke liye apply nahi kar sakte kyunki aap do jagah se fayda nahi le sakte.

5. Help Center par documents verify karwana compulsory hai?

Haan, online form bharna aur lock karna sirf pehla step hai. Jab tak aap Help Center ja kar physical copy verify nahi karwayenge, aapki application processing aage nahi badhegi.
