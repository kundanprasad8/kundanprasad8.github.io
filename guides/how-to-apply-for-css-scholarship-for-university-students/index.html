How to Apply for CSS Scholarship for University Students: 2026 Ka Sabse Easy method

Published: 2026-05-19

Kya aap college ya university mein padh rahe hain aur apni padhai ke kharche ke liye ek achhi scholarship ki talash mein hain? Agar aapne Class 12th mein acche marks laye hain, toh Bharat sarkar ki sabse behtar scheme aapka intezar kar rahi hai. Hum baat kar rahe hain Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship (CSS) ki, jise ab PM-USP CSS bhi kaha jata hai.

Bahut se students ko lagta hai ki college scholarship pana bahut mushkil kaam hai. Lekin sach toh ye hai ki agar aapko sahi tarika pata ho, toh aap ghar baithe iske liye online apply kar sakte hain.

CSS Scholarship Kya Hai aur Iske Kya Fayde Hain?

Simple words mein kahein toh ye scholarship Ministry of Education (Bharat Sarkar) dwara di jati hai. Iska maqsad desh ke un honhaar bacho ki madad karna hai jo paise ki kami ki wajah se apni higher studies nahi rokna chahte.

Is scheme ke tahat university aur college students ko har saal ₹12,000 se lekar ₹20,000 tak ki financial help milti hai. Graduation ke dauran aapko ₹12,000 har saal milenge, aur agar aap Post-Graduation (MA, MSc, MBA etc.) karte hain, toh ye rashi ₹20,000 per year ho jati hai. Sabse achhi baat ye hai ki ye paisa direct aapke bank account mein aata hai.

CSS Scholarship Ke Liye Eligibility Criteria

Online apply karne se pehle ye check karna jaruri hai ki kya aap iske liye eligible hain ya nahi. Iske kuch strict rules hain:

  • Top 20th Percentile: Aapne Class 12th (ya equivalent) mein apne respective board (jaise JAC, CBSE, ICSE) ke top 20% students ki list mein jagah banayi ho.
  • Regular Course: Aap kisi recognized college ya university se regular course (Degree, Engineering, Medical) kar rahe hon. Distance learning wale students isme apply nahi kar sakte.
  • Family Income: Aapke parivar ki kul varshik aay (Annual Income) ₹4.5 Lakh se zyada nahi honi chahiye.
  • No Other Scholarship: Aap kisi dusri sarkari scholarship (jaise e-Kalyan ya state schemes) ka fayda na le rahe hon.

Required Documents (Form Bharne Se Pehle Taiyar Rakhein)

Form beech mein na atke, isliye in documents ko pehle se scan karke rakh lein:

  • Class 12th ki Marksheet
  • Aadhaar Card (Aapke bank account se linked hona chahiye)
  • Current College/University ki Fee Receipt ya Admission Letter
  • Valid Income Certificate (Pita ke naam par)
  • Caste Certificate (Agar applicable ho)
  • Bonafide Student Certificate (Ye aapko aapke college se milega)

How to Apply for CSS Scholarship for University Students: Step by Step Process

Is scholarship ka pura process National Scholarship Portal (NSP) ke zariye online hota hai. Chaliye pure process ko aasan steps mein samajhte hain.

Step 1: NSP OTR Registration Complete Karein

Ab direct apply nahi hota. Sabse pehle aapko apne mobile par NSP OTR App aur AadhaarFaceRD App download karni hogi. Wahan apna Aadhaar aur mobile number verify karke 14-digit ka OTR (One-Time Registration) ID generate karein.

Step 2: NSP Portal Par Login Karein

OTR ID aur password milne ke baad, official website (scholarships.gov.in) par jayein. Wahan login button par click karke apni ID aur password enter karein. First time login par password change karne ko kahega, toh ek achha sa password set kar lein.

Step 3: Application Form Bharein

Login hote hi aapka dashboard khul jayega. 'Application Form' par click karein. Yahan aapko teen tarah ki details bharni hongi:

  • General Details: Aapka naam, DOB, aur religion.
  • Academic Details: Aapke 12th ke marks, roll number, board ka naam, aur abhi aap jis college/university mein padh rahe hain uski details.
  • Other Details: Aapke parents ka occupation aur annual income.

Step 4: Scheme Select Karein

Jab aap apni saari details dhang se bhar denge, toh system aapki eligibility ke hisab se schemes show karein. Wahan aapko "Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students" par tick karna hai.

Step 5: Documents Upload Aur Submit Karein

Ab apne documents (Bonafide certificate, income certificate etc.) clear format mein upload karein. Ek baar pure form ko dobara check kar lein ki kahin koi spelling mistake toh nahi hai. Sab sahi hone par 'Final Submit' button par click kar dein aur printout nikal lein.

Selection Process Aur Paisa Kab Aata Hai?

Form bharne ke baad verification ka kaam shuru hota hai. Pehle aapka college verify karta hai, fir state board ya ministry verify karti hai. Iske baad ek final merit list banti hai. Agar aapka naam us merit list mein aa jata hai, toh scholarship ka paisa seedhe aapke Aadhaar seeded bank account mein bhej diya jata hai.

FAQs: Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

1. Kya har saal naya form bharna padta hai? Nahi, aapko sirf first year mein 'Fresh' application bharni hoti hai. Uske baad agle saalo mein aapko sirf 'Renewal' form bharna padega, jisme 50% marks maintain karna jaruri hota hai.

2. Top 20th percentile ka cutoff kaise pata chalega? Har saal aapka state board (jaise JAC ya CBSE) apni website par CSS scholarship ke liye ek cutoff list nikalta hai. Agar aapke marks usse zyada hain, toh aap eligible hain.

3. Kya main B.Tech ya MBBS ke liye isme apply kar sakta hoon? Haan, bilkul. Agar aap kisi recognized university ya institute se professional courses (jaise B.Tech, MBBS, BBA) kar rahe hain, toh bhi aap iske liye pure tarike se eligible hain.

4. Agar bank account Aadhaar se link nahi hai toh kya hoga? Agar aapka account Aadhaar se seed nahi hai, toh aapka transaction fail ho jayega kyunki sarkari paisa ab sirf DBT ke jariye hi bheja jata hai. Isliye bank ja kar ise turant link karayein.

5. Kya main e-Kalyan aur CSS dono ka form bhar sakta hoon? Nahi, aap dono form bhar toh sakte hain, lekin verify sirf ek hi hoga. Agar aap do jagah se scholarship lenge toh aapka form reject ho sakta hai aur action bhi liya ja sakta hai.
