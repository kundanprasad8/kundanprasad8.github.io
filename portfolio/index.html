About me

Not just a website — a system that brings business.

I'm a WordPress and Wix developer based in Ranchi, India, with 5+ years of experience building websites for salons, clinics, cafes, institutes, transport businesses and startups — in India and abroad.

Most businesses don't need a fancier website. They need a website that loads fast, looks trustworthy on a phone, shows up on Google, and turns a visitor into a call or a WhatsApp message. That's what I build — and I don't stop at launch. Every project is backed by lead generation and social media management, so the site keeps working after it goes live.

I run the same systems on my own projects: an automated results platform with 465+ pages, and a blog with 150+ articles. If a system can't hold up on my own sites, I don't sell it to a client.
