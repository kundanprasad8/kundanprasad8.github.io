Exam Age Eligibility Calculator

Check whether your age fits a government exam's limits. Pick the exam, enter your date of birth, and see instantly if you qualify — with the exact age calculation shown.

Age Eligibility Check

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth from your Class 10 certificate.
  2. Choose the exam you want to check.
  3. Set the cut-off date from the official notification (we pre-filled a common one).
  4. Press Check — the tool shows your exact age and eligibility.

Formula Explained

How it works: the tool computes your age on the notification's cut-off date and compares it with the exam's minimum and maximum. Upper-age relaxation for reserved categories (usually 3–5 years) is not auto-applied — add it yourself per the notification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which date counts for age eligibility?

The cut-off date printed in the official notification (often 1 January or 1 August of the exam year). Your birth date must fall inside the notified range.

Is there age relaxation for OBC/SC/ST?

Yes — typically 3 years for OBC and 5 years for SC/ST in central exams; state exams publish their own tables. Always read the notification's relaxation chart.

What if I am over-age by a few months?

Even one day over the maximum on the cut-off date disqualifies you. There is no rounding or grace.

Do I need the same DOB proof everywhere?

Your Class 10 certificate's date of birth is the reference document for almost all government exams — keep it consistent.

Can I apply if I am under 18?

Most graduate-level exams need 18+ or 20/21+; Agniveer starts at 17.5. Check the specific exam's minimum.
