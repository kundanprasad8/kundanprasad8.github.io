Frequently Asked Questions

Are these the official 2027 exam dates?

No — these are the usual months based on previous cycles. Conducting bodies release exact dates in their own notifications; always confirm on the official website before planning travel or leave.

When do 2027 board exams start?

CBSE, ICSE and most state boards begin Class 10/12 theory exams in mid-February. Date sheets appear in December–January.

Which is the biggest exam of 2027 by applicants?

By volume: board exams (crores), then NEET UG and JEE Main (20+ lakh each), then SSC and railway recruitments (tens of lakhs per cycle).

Where can I download admit cards?

Only from the conducting body's official website, usually 7–15 days before the exam. Never pay anyone for an admit card.

How do I get notified when a 2027 date is announced?

Bookmark this page — we update it as official notifications release — and follow the exam's official portal for the notification PDF.
