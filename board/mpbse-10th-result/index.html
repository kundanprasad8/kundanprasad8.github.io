MPBSE HSC (Class 10) Result

The Madhya Pradesh Board of Secondary Education (MPBSE) conducts the HSC (Class 10) examinations for schools affiliated in Madhya Pradesh. Lakhs of students appear every year, and the result is one of the most searched education updates in the state. This page tracks the MPBSE HSC (Class 10) result — expected date, official websites, step-by-step checking process, and what to do after the result.

Result date tracker: the board has not announced this year's exact date yet. Based on previous years, the MPBSE HSC (Class 10) result is expected in April–May. The official date is announced on mpbse.nic.in a few days before declaration.

How to Check the MPBSE HSC (Class 10) Result

  1. Go to the official result portal: mpbse.nic.in (type it directly; do not trust forwarded links).
  2. Find the link titled "MPBSE HSC (Class 10) Result" on the homepage.
  3. Enter your roll number and date of birth exactly as on your admit card.
  4. Your subject-wise marks and overall result appear on screen — download or print the page.
  5. Collect the original marksheet from your school when it arrives; the online copy is provisional.

Other Ways to Get Your Result

  • DigiLocker: digital marksheets appear in the DigiLocker app under Education → your board. Valid for admissions and job applications.
  • SMS: the board notifies an SMS format (for example, a keyword sent to a short code) on result day for areas with weak internet.
  • School notice board: schools receive the full result list and display it; your class teacher can confirm your marks.

Details on Your HSC (Class 10) Marksheet

Check these carefully when the result opens: your name spelling, roll number, date of birth, subject-wise theory and practical marks, total, percentage/CGPA, division, and the qualifying status (pass / compartment / fail). Report any error to your school within the correction window.

After the Result: Revaluation and Compartment

If your marks are lower than expected, apply for rechecking/revaluation within the board's window (usually 7–15 days, per-subject fee applies). If you did not clear one or two subjects, compartment exams are held a few weeks later — your school will guide the application. Students who clear all subjects can move to Class 11 admissions (for HSC (Class 10) Class 10) or college and entrance-exam applications (for HSC (Class 10) Class 12) right away using the provisional marksheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the MPBSE HSC (Class 10) result be declared?

Based on previous years, the Madhya Pradesh Board of Secondary Education usually declares the HSC (Class 10) result in April–May. The board announces the exact date and time a few days in advance through a press note on mpbse.nic.in. This page is updated as soon as the date is announced.

Where can I check the MPBSE HSC (Class 10) result?

On the board's official website (mpbse.nic.in), through the DigiLocker app, and via SMS on the number notified by the board. Avoid unofficial sites that ask for money or personal details.

What do I need to check my HSC (Class 10) result?

Your roll number and usually your date of birth or school code, exactly as printed on your admit card. Keep the admit card ready before result day.

Is the online HSC (Class 10) marksheet valid for admission?

The online result is provisional. Colleges accept it for provisional admission, but you must submit the original marksheet and passing certificate issued by your school once available.

What if I fail in one or two subjects in MPBSE HSC (Class 10)?

Most boards, including MPBSE, hold compartment (supplementary) exams a few weeks after the main result. Check the compartment date sheet on the board website and apply through your school before the deadline.

How do I apply for revaluation of my HSC (Class 10) answer sheets?

The board opens a short rechecking/revaluation window after the result — usually 7 to 15 days. Apply online on mpbse.nic.in, pay the per-subject fee, and track the revised marks on the same portal.

My name or date of birth is wrong on the HSC (Class 10) marksheet. What now?

Contact your school office immediately. Name and date-of-birth corrections go through the school to the board, and there is a fixed correction window — do not delay.

How can I download my HSC (Class 10) marksheet from DigiLocker?

Install the DigiLocker app, sign in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number, go to the Education section, select your board and class, and enter your roll number. The digital marksheet is valid for most official purposes.
